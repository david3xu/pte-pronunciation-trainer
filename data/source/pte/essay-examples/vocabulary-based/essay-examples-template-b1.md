# PTE Essay Examples - Template B1 Collection

**Template Structure:** Positive → Negative (Mostly Agree)

**Target:** 200-300 words | Simple + Academic Terms

---

## 📋 Rules for Fill-in Terms

### ✅ Must Follow:
1. **Simple vocabulary** - Use B1-B2 level words (avoid complex/rare words)
2. **Academic tone** - Sound formal, not casual (avoid slang)
3. **Short phrases** - Maximum 2-4 words per fill-in term
4. **Easy to speak** - Must flow naturally when reading aloud
5. **Easy to remember** - Don't use overly complicated combinations
6. **Use topic keywords** - Main topic comes directly from the question
7. **Word count** - Final essay must be 200-300 words (aim for 220-230)

### ❌ Avoid:
- ❌ Long compound phrases (5+ words)
- ❌ Complex academic jargon (unless in question)
- ❌ Difficult pronunciation combinations
- ❌ Repetitive/redundant pairings ("assessment and evaluation")

### Examples:
- ✅ GOOD: "fair assessment", "test stress", "financial difficulties"
- ❌ TOO LONG: "objective assessment and fair evaluation system"
- ❌ TOO COMPLEX: "socioeconomic ramifications and implications"

## 📝 Template B1 (Mostly Agree)

**Use when:** Benefits > Challenges | You mostly AGREE

### Structure Overview:
1. **Introduction** - State your mostly positive position
2. **Paragraph 2** - POSITIVE arguments (advantages)
3. **Paragraph 3** - Choose ONE:
   - **Option A:** Negative challenges (standard agree/disagree)
   - **Option B:** Alternative benefits (when question compares two approaches)
   - **Option C:** Solutions/recommendations (when question asks for solutions)
4. **Conclusion** - Reaffirm that advantages remain significant

---
