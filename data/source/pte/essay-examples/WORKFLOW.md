# Essay Creation Workflow - Quick Reference

**Purpose:** Step-by-step process for creating new PTE essay answers using Template B
**Target Score:** 25-26/26 (proven system with 20 existing examples)
**Time Required:** 15-20 minutes per essay

---

## The Golden Rule

✅ **ALWAYS strictly follow Template B** (155 base words)
✅ **ALWAYS use vocabulary from essay-topic-vocabulary.md first**
✅ **IF you create new terms → MUST update essay-topic-vocabulary.md**

---

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Extract Topic Keywords (2 minutes)

**Task:** Read the prompt 2x and extract 8-12 keywords

**What to extract:**
- ✅ Nouns (main concepts)
- ✅ Adjectives (descriptors)
- ✅ Verbs (actions)
- ✅ Multi-word phrases (exact phrases from prompt)

**Example:**
```
Topic: "Travel in education - is it necessary?"

Keywords Extracted:
1. travel
2. education
3. necessary
4. scholar
5. home
6. quality
7. value / valuable
8. component
```

**Where to record:** Start of your essay document under `## 1. Words from Topic`

---

**Task:** Pick 8-10 moderate academic terms from essay-topic-vocabulary.md

**Selection Strategy:**

- Pick 5-7 terms that match your topic
- Prefer 2-3 word phrases over single words

**From Section 8 - Universal Academic (3-4 terms):**

- potential to facilitate
- positive effects / negative effects
- substantial influence

**From Section 9 - Transitions (use these in ALL essays):**
- to begin with
- in particular
- however
- taken together
- thus

**Example for "Travel in Education":**

### Step 4: Fill Template B Placeholders (10 minutes)

**Task:** Write the 4-paragraph essay following Template B exactly

#### Paragraph 1 - Introduction (28 base words)

**Template:**
```
In recent years, the topic of [keywords] has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion.

I contend that [your position], and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.
```

**How to fill:**
- `[keywords]` → Combine 2-3 topic words
  - Examples: "travel in education", "work-life balance", "building design effects"
- `[your position]` → Use 4-6 topic keywords + 1 moderate vocab word
  - Example: "travel is a **valuable** component of quality education even though scholars succeed without leaving home"

**Output Example:**
```
In recent years, the topic of travel in education has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion.

I contend that travel is a valuable component of quality education even though some scholars achieve success without leaving home, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.
```

---

#### Paragraph 2 - Advantage/Reason (58 base words)

**Template:**
```
To begin with, one compelling [reason/advantage/feature] of [keywords] is its potential to facilitate [keywords].

In particular, [keywords] can foster [keywords], ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as [keywords].

For instance, evidence from [source 1] reveals that approximately 80% of [relevant subjects] experienced [keywords] as a direct result of [keywords].
```

**How to fill:**
- `[reason/advantage/feature]` → Choose ONE: reason OR advantage OR feature
- `[keywords]` → Mix topic words + vocabulary terms
- `[source 1]` → Use simple realistic source
  - Examples: "university research", "workplace studies", "educational institutions"
- `[relevant subjects]` → Context-appropriate group
  - Examples: "students", "employees", "organizations"

**Fill Strategy:**
```
| Placeholder | Fill With | Example |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| [reason/advantage/feature] | advantage | "advantage" |
| [keywords] #1 | Topic words | "educational travel" |
| [keywords] #2 | Vocab terms | "practical learning and cultural awareness" |
| [keywords] #3 | Simple phrase | "visiting different places" |
| [keywords] #4 | Moderate vocab | "direct observation of historical sites" |
| [keywords] #5 | Vocab terms | "deeper understanding and memorable experiences" |
| [source 1] | Simple source | "university research" |
| [relevant subjects] | Topic-related | "students who traveled abroad" |
| [keywords] #6 | Moderate vocab | "improved language skills" |
| [keywords] #7 | Simple phrase | "real-world exposure" |
```

---

#### Paragraph 3 - Challenge/Counterpoint (51 base words)

**Template:**
```
However, it is equally important to acknowledge that [keywords] may also give rise to certain challenges.

This is largely because [keywords] are likely to generate [keywords], which may subsequently cause [keywords].

According to research by [source 2], over 60% of [relevant subjects] have been affected by [keywords] stemming from [keywords].
```

**How to fill:**
- Start with "However, it is equally important to acknowledge that" (STRICT)
- "challenges" can be replaced: "valid arguments", "limitations", "disadvantages" (context-appropriate)
- `[source 2]` → Different from source 1 (vary your sources)
  - Examples: "educational institutions", "organizational studies", "policy research"

**Fill Strategy:**
```
| Placeholder | Fill With | Example |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| [keywords] #1 | Topic words | "staying home for education" |
| [keywords] #2 | Topic + moderate | "dedicated scholars with books" |
| [keywords] #3 | Vocab terms | "deep knowledge through focused study" |
| [keywords] #4 | Topic reasoning | "people to view travel as less necessary" |
| [source 2] | Different source | "educational institutions" |
| [relevant subjects] | Topic-related | "successful scholars" |
| [keywords] #5 | Vocab terms | "academic excellence without travel" |
| [keywords] #6 | Moderate vocab | "disciplined reading and consistent effort" |
```

---

#### Paragraph 4 - Conclusion (43 base words)

**Template:**
```
Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that [rephrase position], as the influence of [keywords] and [keywords] is substantial and far-reaching.

Thus, it is imperative for [relevant subjects] to dedicate adequate [resources/time/efforts/energy/money] to managing this issue effectively.
```

**How to fill:**
- `[rephrase position]` → Restate your intro position using different words
- `[keywords]` → Pick 2 key concepts from paragraphs 2 & 3
- `[resources/time/efforts/energy/money]` → Choose 1-2 appropriate ones

**Fill Strategy:**
```
| Placeholder | Fill With | Example |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| [rephrase position] | Restate intro differently | "both approaches have merit, but travel adds unique value" |
| [keywords] #1 | From para 2 | "real-world experiences" |
| [keywords] #2 | From para 3 | "cross-cultural exposure" |
| [relevant subjects] | Who should act | "students and educational programs" |
| [resources/time/etc] | Pick 1-2 | "time and resources" |
```

### Step 5: Check Word Count (1 minute)

**Target:** 200-300 words

**Formula:**
- Template B base: **155 words**
- Topic keywords fills: **+30-50 words**
- Vocabulary term fills: **+20-40 words**
- Elaboration: **+20-50 words**
- **Total: 225-295 words** ✅

**If under 200 words:**
- Add more elaboration in sentences 2-3 of paragraphs 2-3
- Use longer vocabulary phrases (3-4 words instead of 2)

**If over 300 words:**
- Reduce elaboration
- Use shorter vocabulary terms (2 words instead of 3-4)

### Step 6: Create New Terms (If Needed) + Update Vocab File (2 minutes)

**When to create new terms:**
- Topic requires specific terminology not in essay-topic-vocabulary.md
- You need moderate academic words that aren't listed

**New Term Guidelines:**
✅ **Keep 2-3 words max** (easy to memorize)
✅ **Moderate difficulty** (not "good/bad", not "paradigmatic/synergistic")
✅ **Reusable** (could apply to other essays)

**Example New Terms:**
- digital literacy ← 2 words, moderate, reusable
- remote collaboration ← 2 words, moderate, reusable
- environmental accountability ← 2 words, moderate, reusable

**How to update essay-topic-vocabulary.md:**

1. Open `essay-topic-vocabulary.md`
2. Find the appropriate section (1-9)
3. Add your new term to the list
4. Update the section count
5. Update the total count at bottom

- compelling advantage
- potential to facilitate
- substantial influence

**New Terms Needed:**

- digital workspace (not in vocab file)

**Action:** Will add these to Section 6 (Technology) after writing essay

### Step 4: Fill Template B (10 min)

**Paragraph 1:**
```
In recent years, the topic of remote work versus office work has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion.

I contend that remote work offers compelling advantages for productivity levels and workplace flexibility, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.
```

**Paragraph 2:**
```
To begin with, one compelling advantage of remote work is its potential to facilitate improved work-life balance and productivity levels. In particular, workplace flexibility and reduced commute time can foster enhanced work performance and job satisfaction, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as increased productivity and better mental wellbeing. For instance, evidence from workplace studies reveals that approximately 80% of remote employees experienced higher productivity levels as a direct result of flexible work arrangements and comfortable home environments.
```

**Paragraph 3:**
```
However, it is equally important to acknowledge that office work may also give rise to certain valid arguments. This is largely because traditional workplace environments and direct collaboration are likely to generate stronger team communication and immediate support, which may subsequently cause some employees to prefer in-person interactions for complex projects. According to research by organizational studies, over 60% of managers have observed challenges in remote collaboration stemming from reduced face-to-face interaction and workplace coordination difficulties.
```

**Paragraph 4:**
```
Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that remote work provides substantial advantages for individual productivity and work-life balance, as the influence of workplace flexibility and personal comfort is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for employers and employees to dedicate adequate resources and technology to managing remote work arrangements effectively.
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ DON'T:
- Deviate from Template B structure
- Use overly complex vocabulary ("paradigmatic", "synergistic")
- Use overly simple words ("very good", "very bad", "help")
- Make up specific sources ("Annual Report of XYZ Organization 2023")
- Use first-person stories ("When I was in university...")
- Forget to update vocabulary file when adding new terms
- Copy existing essays word-for-word (use as reference only)

### ✅ DO:
- Follow Template B exactly (155 base words)
- Reuse topic keywords 5+ times
- Use moderate academic vocabulary
- Use simple realistic sources (university research, workplace studies)
- Keep new terms 2-3 words max
- Update essay-topic-vocabulary.md immediately when adding terms
- Document your fill strategy for future reference
