# PTE Essay Example - Climate Change Responsibility

## Topic
> Climate change is a concerning global issue. Who should take the responsibilities: governments, big companies or individuals?

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of climate change issues and responsibility allocation has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that addressing climate change requires shared responsibilities among governments, big companies, and individuals, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling reason why governments should take responsibility is its potential to facilitate policy frameworks and environmental protection. In particular, governments can foster binding agreements and conservation efforts through environmental responsibility and resource management, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as reduced carbon emissions and improved sustainable practices. For instance, policy research reveals that approximately 80% of countries with strong government cooperation experienced significant environmental benefits as a direct result of coordinated climate change initiatives.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that big companies and individuals may also give rise to substantial contributions. This is largely because business activities generate significant pollution levels and resource depletion, while individual lifestyle choices are likely to affect environmental damage, which may subsequently cause both companies and individuals to share environmental responsibility. According to research by environmental institutions, over 60% of carbon emissions stemming from business operations and personal consumption patterns.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that climate change requires collective action, as the influence of government policies, corporate environmental responsibility, and individual sustainable solutions is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for governments, companies, and individuals to dedicate adequate resources and commitment to managing this issue effectively.
