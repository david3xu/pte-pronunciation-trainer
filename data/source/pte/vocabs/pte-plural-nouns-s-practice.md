# PTE Plural Nouns - Final /s/ Practice

### Vocabulary for 's' Sound Practice

**Mastered**: 0/82

**Key Rule**: Final -s after voiceless consonants (/p/, /t/, /k/, /f/, /θ/) = breath, no voice. Vocal cords should NOT vibrate.

## Practice Tips

| Ending | Sound | Examples |
|--------|-------|----------|
| -ts | /ts/ | students, facts, streets |
| -ks | /ks/ | books, desks, snacks |
| -ps | /ps/ | cups, shops, laptops |
| -θs | /θs/ | months |

**Practice Method**: Place your hand on your throat. When pronouncing the final /s/, you should feel NO vibration - only airflow.
