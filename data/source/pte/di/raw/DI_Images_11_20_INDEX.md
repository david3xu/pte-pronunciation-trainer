## 🔊 KEY PRONUNCIATION CHALLENGES IN #11-20

### Challenge 1: Energy Terms
- ✅ "megawatt hours" = MEG-uh-wot OW-urz (3 words, NOT run together)
- ✅ "kilowatt hours" = KIL-uh-wot OW-urz (3 words)
- ✅ "billion" = BIL-yun (NOT "bill-ee-on")

### Challenge 2: Price Expressions
- ✅ "ONE hundred and FORTY dollars" (multiple stresses)
- ✅ "TWO hundred and FIFTY thousand" (four stresses)
- ❌ NOT "one-hundred-forty" (run together)

### Challenge 3: Geographic Names
- ✅ "Beijing" = bay-JING (stress second)
- ❌ NOT "BAY-jing" (stress first)
- ✅ "Netherlands" = NETH-ur-landz

### Challenge 4: Time Expressions
- ✅ "TWELVE noon" (two words, both stressed)
- ✅ "FOUR AM" = FOR AY-EM (spell out)
- ✅ "FOUR PM" = FOR PEE-EM (spell out)

### Challenge 5: Ratio Expressions
- ✅ "TWENTY-THREE to TWENTY-FOUR" (four stresses)
- ❌ NOT "twenty-three-to-twenty-four" (flat)

### Challenge 6: Temperature
- ✅ "MINUS five degrees" (clear "minus")
- ✅ "Celsius" = SEL-see-us
- ✅ "temperature" = TEM-pur-uh-chur (4 syllables, stress FIRST)
