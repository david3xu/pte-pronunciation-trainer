## 🔊 PRONUNCIATION PATTERNS TO MASTER

### Years (Critical for Images #1, #3, #5, #7, #8, #9)
- ✅ 1700 = "SEVENTEEN hundred" (NOT "one seven zero zero")
- ✅ 1953 = "NINETEEN fifty-three" (NOT "one nine five three")
- ✅ 2002 = "TWO thousand and TWO" (NOT "two zero zero two")
- ✅ 2101 = "TWENTY-one oh-one" (for future years)

### Numbers with Decimals (Image #10)
- ✅ 16.2% = "SIXTEEN point TWO percent"
- ✅ 0.7% = "ZERO point SEVEN percent"

### Large Numbers (Images #1, #5, #8, #9)
- ✅ 2,200 = "TWO thousand TWO hundred"
- ✅ 70 million = "SEVENTY million"
- ✅ 130 kg = "ONE hundred and THIRTY kilograms"

### Currency (Image #2)
- ✅ Dirhams = "DEER-humz"

### Country Names (Image #9)
- ✅ Brazil = "bruh-ZIL"
- ✅ Ethiopia = "ee-thee-OH-pee-uh"
- ✅ Liberia = "ly-BEER-ee-uh"
