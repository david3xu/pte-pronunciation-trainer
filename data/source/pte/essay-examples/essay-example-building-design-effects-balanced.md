# PTE Essay Example - Building Design Effects (Vocabulary Method)

## Topic
> How does the design of buildings affect, either positively or negatively, where people work and live?

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 249** | **Template: B** | **Method: Vocabulary File Only**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of building design effects on the living environment and the working environment has emerged as a prominent subject of academic discussion. I contend that architectural features exert substantial influence on residential areas and commercial districts, and the following analysis presents arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of well-designed buildings is their potential to facilitate quality of life for people choosing where to live and work. In particular, natural light access, ventilation systems, and public spaces create living environment and working environment conditions that foster positive effects such as improved environmental quality and lifestyle choices. For instance, studies indicate that approximately 80% of people prefer residential areas containing well-designed buildings as a direct result of spatial design guided by urban planning studies.

However, it is important to acknowledge significant disadvantages associated with poorly designed structures. This is largely because limited spatial design and physical infrastructure generate negative effects that cause individuals to avoid certain residential areas or commercial districts. According to research, over 60% of workers relocate from working environments when building design effects restrict ventilation systems and public spaces.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that building design effects remain a vital component in determining where people live and work because architectural features can provide positive effects or negative effects. Thus, it is imperative for policy frameworks and urban planning studies to prioritize well-designed buildings that deliver sustainable development for future residential areas and commercial districts.
