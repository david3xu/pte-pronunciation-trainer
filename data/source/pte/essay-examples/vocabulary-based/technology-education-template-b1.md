# PTE Essay Example - Technology in Education (Template B1 - Positive→Negative)

## Topic
> Some people believe that technology has improved education, while others think it has made things worse. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 272** | **Template: B1** | **Approach: Universal Vocabulary**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of technology in education has emerged as a significant subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that while technology brings substantial benefits to learning, it also presents certain challenges, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of technology in education is its potential to facilitate practical learning and deeper understanding. In particular, students using educational technology can foster improved performance and real-world exposure, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as enhanced skills and better academic results. For instance, evidence from educational research reveals that approximately 75% of students who used technology in classrooms experienced improved performance as a direct result of interactive learning tools and digital resources.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that technology in education may also give rise to certain challenges. This is largely because excessive technology use is likely to generate constant pressure and reduced focus, which may subsequently cause mental wellbeing issues and decreased attention spans. According to research by educational institutions, over 58% of students have been affected by concentration difficulties resulting from digital distractions and screen time.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that while technology in education offers significant benefits for learning and skill development, it also creates challenges that must be managed carefully, as the influence of balanced technology use and proper guidance is substantial and meaningful. Thus, it is essential for educators and students to dedicate adequate time and careful planning to managing this issue effectively.

## Why Template B1 (Positive→Negative)?

### **Choice of Structure:**
This topic asks to "discuss both views" where:
- One view: technology **improved** education (positive)
- Other view: technology made things **worse** (negative)

**Template B1 is perfect** because:
- P2 = Benefits first (improved education) ✅
- P3 = Challenges second (made things worse) ✅
- Matches the prompt order naturally

### **Compare to Template B2:**
If we used B2 (negative→positive):
- P2 = Challenges first
- P3 = Benefits second
- Still works, but feels less natural for this prompt

## Template B1 vs B2 - When to Use

| Prompt Type | Best Template | Why |
|------------|---------------|-----|
| "Discuss advantages and disadvantages" | **B1** | Start with advantages (expected) |
| "Has X improved Y?" (you agree) | **B1** | Emphasize benefits first |
| "To what extent is X a problem?" | **B2** | Problem-focused = start negative |
| "Is X foolish/wrong?" (balanced view) | **B2** | Address criticism first |
| "Discuss both views" (positive mentioned first) | **B1** | Follow prompt order |
