# How to Write a 90+ PTE Essay

This guide shows you how to upgrade from a passing essay (65-75) to a top-band score (90+) using a structured template approach with academic but simple vocabulary.

---

## Session 1: Understanding 90+ Requirements

### What Separates 90+ from 68-74?

**Common Score: 68-74**
- ❌ Vague research citations: "evidence shows...", "according to research..."
- ❌ High topic word repetition (8-11 times)
- ❌ Generic examples: "many people experienced..."
- ❌ Template-obvious introduction: "In recent years, the topic of X has emerged..."

**Top Score: 90+**
- ✅ Concrete observations (no fake research)
- ✅ Topic paraphrasing (reduce repetition to 6-7 times)
- ✅ Specific realistic details
- ✅ Natural academic introduction

---

### Critical Mistakes to Avoid (0-Score Risks)

#### 1. Generic Statements with No Topic Connection

**❌ WRONG:**
> "There are many arguments that are in favour of my belief."

This could appear in ANY essay - instant penalty.

**✅ CORRECT:**
> Always tie statements to your specific topic with concrete reasoning.

#### 2. Fake Research Citations

**❌ WRONG:**
> "According to research by workplace experts, 80% of employees have experienced..."

**✅ CORRECT:**
> "Employees who have protected personal time demonstrate stronger family relationships and greater job satisfaction."

(State the observation directly - no need to cite non-existent research)

#### 3. Keyword Stacking Without Logic

**❌ WRONG:**
> Using a template like: "X will bring about X and generate X leading to X"

This becomes mindless keyword repetition.

**✅ CORRECT:**
> Use verified logic chains: Cause → Effect → Result (with clear connections)

---

### The 90+ Template Structure

#### Introduction
```
The question of [topic restatement] has attracted considerable attention in modern society.
I contend that [topic paraphrase 1] is [position statement] for [subject] despite
[acknowledgment], and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments
supporting this viewpoint.
```

**Why this works:**
- ✅ Academic phrase: "attracted considerable attention"
- ✅ Simple vocabulary (B1/B2 level)
- ✅ Universal - works for any topic
- ✅ Natural sounding (not obviously templated)

**Paraphrasing Rule for [topic restatement]:**

**KEEP (don't change):**
- Key technical terms (specific to the topic)
- Core concept words (define what the question is about)

**PARAPHRASE (change to synonyms):**
- Generic verbs (involve → include, make → create)
- Common nouns (employers → organizations, people → individuals)
- General phrases (can be restructured)

**Example:**
- **Question:** "employers **involve** employees in the **decision-making** of **products**"
- **KEEP:** "decision-making", "products" (key concepts)
- **CHANGE:** "employers" → "organizations", "involve" → "include"
- **Result:** "organizations should include staff in decision-making about products" ✓

#### Body Paragraph 2 (Benefits/Importance)
```
To begin with, one compelling advantage of [topic] is its potential to bring about
[outcome 1]. In particular, [topic paraphrase 2] can facilitate [outcome 2],
ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as [outcome 3].

[Subject group] who [specific action] demonstrate [concrete results]. [OPTIONAL:
This [approach/practice/method] also [contributes to/enhances/supports] [additional
benefit aspect].] For instance, [specific subjects] with [specific condition] report
significantly [evidence result] compared to those who [contrasting behavior].
```

**Key principle:** Replace vague "research shows" with concrete "Subject + Action + Result"

**Paragraph sizing tip:** If your position favors one side (e.g., "substantial benefits despite certain limitations"), allocate more words to the favored paragraph. Target 75-80 words for your main argument, 60-65 words for the counterpoint.

#### Body Paragraph 3 (Challenges/Barriers)
```
However, it is equally important to acknowledge that [topic paraphrase 3] faces
certain challenges in modern society. This is largely because [root cause] is likely
to generate [intermediate effect], which may subsequently lead to [direct obstacle].

[Specific context] that [behavior pattern] create pressure to [concrete action example].
As a result, many [subjects] experience [obstacle manifestation] that make it difficult
to maintain [topic paraphrase 4].
```

**Key principle:** Concrete details ("respond to emails after hours") instead of generic claims ("high pressure")

#### Conclusion
```
Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of [topic],
since its advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany it. Thus,
it is essential for [stakeholders] to allocate sufficient resources to address these
challenges effectively.
```

---

### Topic Paraphrasing Strategy (Reduce Repetition)

**You need 4-5 paraphrase variations** to avoid excessive repetition:

**Example: "work-life balance"**
1. "work-life balance" (first use)
2. "this equilibrium"
3. "achieving this balance"
4. "maintaining this balance"
5. "a balanced lifestyle"

**Result:** Topic term appears only 6-7 times instead of 11

---

### Logic Chain Verification

**Before writing, map your causal logic:**

#### Para 2 (Benefits) Chain:
```
CLAIM: Topic → Outcome 1 → Outcome 2 → Outcome 3

EVIDENCE: [Subject group] demonstrates:
          - Result A (supports Outcome 1 ✓)
          - Result B (supports Outcome 3 ✓)
```

#### Para 3 (Challenges) Chain:
```
CLAIM: Root Cause → Intermediate Effect → Direct Obstacle → Difficulty

EVIDENCE: [Specific context] shows:
          - Observable behavior (supports Direct Obstacle ✓)
```

**Critical Rule:** Each arrow must be logical cause → effect (no backwards causality)

---

### Evidence Without Fake Citations

#### Wrong Approach
❌ "According to research from [vague source], [percentage]% of [subjects]..."

#### Correct Approach (3 Techniques)

**1. Direct Observation:**
> "Employees who have clear boundaries between work hours and personal life demonstrate better psychological well-being."

**2. Specific Context:**
> "Corporate environments that reward constant availability create pressure to respond to emails after hours."

**3. Comparative Statement:**
> "Professionals with protected personal time report significantly better well-being compared to those who routinely work late."

---

### Vocabulary Level: Academic but Simple

**Target:** B1/B2 (not B2/C1)

**Good Examples:**
- ✅ "attracted considerable attention" (B2 but easy)
- ✅ "demonstrate" (B1)
- ✅ "facilitate" (B2 but common)
- ✅ "pressure to" (B1)

**Too Complex:**
- ❌ "mitigate challenges"
- ❌ "exacerbate difficulties"
- ❌ "ameliorate conditions"

**Too Casual:**
- ❌ "got a lot of interest"
- ❌ "makes things better"
- ❌ "causes problems"

---

### Quick Checklist for 90+

Before submitting, verify:

**Content:**
- [ ] No fake research citations ("according to studies...")
- [ ] Concrete, specific details (not "many people...")
- [ ] Logic chains verified (cause → effect makes sense)

**Language:**
- [ ] Topic term appears 6-7 times (not 10+)
- [ ] 4-5 paraphrase variations used
- [ ] Academic but simple vocabulary (B1/B2)
- [ ] Natural introduction (not "In recent years, the topic of X...")

**Structure:**
- [ ] Clear position stated in intro
- [ ] Each paragraph has logical flow
- [ ] Evidence supports claims (not just placeholder text)
- [ ] 200-250 word count

### Common Questions

**Q: Can I still use a template?**
A: YES! Templates are fine. What's penalized is GENERIC CONTENT within templates (e.g., "there are many reasons"), not the template structure itself.

**Q: How many times can I repeat the topic word?**
A: 6-7 times is safe with paraphrases. Official PTE rubric penalizes "repeating language from the prompt" (copying), not repeating your own paraphrased term.

**Q: Do I need real research?**
A: NO! State observations as facts. "Employees who X demonstrate Y" is better than "Research shows employees experienced Y."

## Session 2: Complete Example - Employee Decision-Making

### Question
"In some companies, some employers involve employees in the decision-making process of products and services. What are the advantages and disadvantages of such a policy?"

---

### Logic Chains

**Para 2 (Advantages):**
```
CLAIM: Employee participation → Higher employee loyalty & stronger engagement
                          → Better business suggestions & creative ideas
                          → Increased revenue & improved satisfaction

EVIDENCE: Companies demonstrate:
          - Greater motivation & stronger sense of belonging (Observable result)
          - Better suggestions & higher revenue (Measured outcome)
          (Validates that engagement leads to suggestions/revenue ✓)
```

**Para 3 (Disadvantages):**
```
CLAIM: Extensive consultation → Workplace conflicts & disagreements
                          → Slower decision processes & reduced efficiency

EVIDENCE: Corporate settings show:
          - Requirement for consensus pressure to hold longer meetings
          - Communication difficulties
          (Validates that consultation causes delay/inefficiency ✓)
```

---

### Complete Essay (238 words)

The question of **whether organizations should involve employees in the decision-making process** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **this collaborative approach** **offers substantial benefits** for **companies** despite **certain limitations**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **employee participation in decision-making** is its potential to bring about **higher employee loyalty and stronger workplace engagement**. In particular, **involving employees in decisions** can facilitate **better business suggestions and creative ideas**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **increased company revenue and improved customer satisfaction**. **Companies that involve employees in decision-making** demonstrate **greater motivation and a stronger sense of belonging**. For instance, **companies that implement regular feedback sessions with staff** report significantly **better business suggestions and higher revenue** compared to those **with top-down decision structures**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **employee participation** faces certain challenges in **modern business environments**. This is largely because **extensive consultation** is likely to generate **workplace conflicts and disagreements between staff and managers**, which may subsequently lead to **slower decision processes and reduced efficiency**. **Corporate settings** that **require consensus from multiple departments** create pressure to **hold longer meetings**. As a result, many **companies** experience **communication difficulties** that make it difficult to maintain **quick business responses**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of **employee participation in decision-making**, since its advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany it. Thus, it is essential for **companies and managers** to allocate sufficient resources to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 2: Formal Written Examinations

### Question
"The formal written examination can be a valid method to assess students’ learning. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Use your own experience."

### Logic Chains

**Para 2 (Advantages):**
```
CLAIM: Formal written examinations → Fair assessment & equal opportunities
                                 → Clear standards & consistent grading
                                 → Accurate knowledge testing & reliable measurement

EVIDENCE: Educational institutions demonstrate:
          - Improved student performance evaluation (Observable result)
          - Better learning outcomes (Measured outcome)
```

**Para 3 (Disadvantages):**
```
CLAIM: Reliance on exams → Test stress & student anxiety
                       → Reduced creativity & limited critical thinking

EVIDENCE: Academic settings show:
          - Pressure to memorize facts rather than understand concepts
          - Test-related stress
          (Validates that high-stakes exams cause anxiety/surface learning ✓)
```

### Complete Essay

The question of **whether formal written examinations are a valid method to assess students** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **this assessment method** **offers substantial benefits** for **students** despite **certain limitations**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **formal written examinations** is their potential to bring about **fair assessment and equal opportunities for all students**. In particular, **using written exams** can facilitate **clear standards and consistent grading**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **accurate knowledge testing and reliable academic measurement**. **Educational institutions that prioritize written exams** demonstrate **improved student performance evaluation**. For instance, **schools that conduct regular formal examinations with students** report significantly **better learning outcomes and fair grading** compared to those **with subjective assessment methods**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **written assessment** faces certain challenges in **modern educational environments**. This is largely because **reliance on exams** is likely to generate **test stress and student anxiety**, which may subsequently lead to **reduced creativity and limited critical thinking**. **Academic settings** that **focus heavily on exam scores** create pressure to **memorize facts rather than understand concepts**. As a result, many **students** experience **test-related stress** that makes it difficult to maintain **academic progress**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of **formal written examinations**, since their advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany them. Thus, it is essential for **schools and teachers** to allocate sufficient resources to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 3: Travel and Quality Education

### Question
"“The value of travel has been overrated”. It is said that one can be a brilliant scholar without leaving one's home base. Do you think travel is a necessary component of a quality education?"

### Logic Chains

**Para 2 (Advantages of Travel):**
```
CLAIM: Educational travel → Cultural awareness & cross-cultural understanding
                        → International exposure & adaptability
                        → Global perspective & personal growth

EVIDENCE: Students demonstrate:
          - Enhanced critical thinking (Internal growth)
          - Deeper knowledge (Academic result)
```

**Para 3 (Local Education - Proves Travel Not Necessary):**
```
CLAIM: Local education alone achieves academic excellence
                        → Access to established institutions
                        → Quality learning in familiar environment
                        → Academic success without travel

EVIDENCE: Learners demonstrate:
          - Academic success and scholarly expertise
          - Through local education and dedicated study
          (Validates that travel is NOT necessary for excellence ✓)
```

### Complete Essay (237 words)

The question of **whether travel is a necessary component of a quality education** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that while **educational travel** **offers substantial benefits** for **students**, it is not strictly necessary for achieving **academic excellence**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **educational travel** is its potential to bring about **cultural awareness and cross-cultural understanding**. In particular, **experiencing new cultures** can facilitate **international exposure and adaptability**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **global perspective and personal growth**. **Students who study abroad** demonstrate **enhanced critical thinking**. For instance, **universities that encourage exchange programs for students** report significantly **deeper knowledge and greater adaptability** compared to those **with local-only curriculums**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **high-quality education** can be achieved without travel through **dedicated local education**. This is largely because **studying locally** provides **access to established institutions** and **full academic resources**, which enable **quality learning in a familiar environment**. **Learners** who **focus on local education and dedicated study** achieve **academic success and scholarly expertise**, demonstrating that one can indeed become a **brilliant scholar** without leaving one's **home base**. As a result, many **students** experience **excellent academic outcomes** through **local education**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that while **educational travel** offers **valuable cultural enrichment**, it is not strictly necessary for **academic excellence**, since **local education** can also produce **scholarly success**. Thus, **students** should view travel as an **enriching option** rather than an **essential requirement**.

## Example 4: Climate Change Responsibility

### Question
"Climate change is a concerning global issue. Who should take the responsibilities: governments, big companies or individuals?"

---

### Logic Chains

**Para 2 (Why Governments Should Lead):**
```
CLAIM: Government leadership → Environmental laws & national regulations
                            → Public funding & policy coordination
                            → Strong enforcement & climate targets

EVIDENCE: Governments demonstrate:
          - Significant pollution reduction
          - Improved air quality
          (Validates that government action produces results ✓)
```

**Para 3 (Others Can Contribute):**
```
CLAIM: While governments lead, companies & individuals contribute
                            → Company efforts & private actions
                            → Environmental improvements
                            → Public awareness & lifestyle changes

EVIDENCE: Climate programs show:
          - Business support & citizen participation
          (Validates that collective action helps ✓)
```

---

### Complete Essay (235 words)

The question of **who should take responsibility for climate change** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **governments should take primary responsibility** for addressing **climate change**, with **valuable supporting roles from companies and individuals**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling reason why **government-led climate action** is essential lies in the unique ability to bring about **environmental laws and national regulations**. In particular, **governments leading climate initiatives** can facilitate **public funding and policy coordination**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **strong enforcement and ambitious climate targets**. **Nations that prioritize climate action** demonstrate **strong environmental regulations**. For instance, **countries with strict environmental policies** report significantly **reduced pollution levels and improved air quality** compared to those **without government action**.

However, it is important to acknowledge that while **government responsibility** is essential, **companies and individuals** also provide **valuable supporting contributions to climate action**. This is largely because **business environmental actions and individual changes** can generate **meaningful environmental improvements**, which may enable **increased public awareness and sustainable behavior**. **Climate initiatives** that **combine government policy with business support** achieve **broader community engagement**. As a result, many **successful climate programs** experience **enhanced effectiveness** through **collective action from all groups**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **governments should take primary responsibility** for **climate change**, while **private sector and individual contributions** enhance overall effectiveness. Thus, it is essential for **governments** to take the lead in addressing this global challenge while encouraging **participation from all sectors of society**.
