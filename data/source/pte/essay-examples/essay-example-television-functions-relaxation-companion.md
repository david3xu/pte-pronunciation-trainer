# PTE Essay Example - Television Functions: Relaxation and Companionship

## Topic
> Television has many functions to play in everyone's life. For some it's relaxation; for some, it is the companion. To what extent do you think the statement is true? Please provide your argument and supporting evidence from your own experience.

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 268** | **Template: B** | **Method: Vocabulary File Only**

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of television's role in entertainment and daily life has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that the statement about television providing relaxation and companionship holds considerable validity, as entertainment programs serve multiple beneficial functions for individual wellbeing, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of television as a source of relaxation is its potential to facilitate stress reduction and improved quality of life. In particular, entertainment programs and leisure activities through media consumption can foster emotional wellbeing and mental peace, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as enhanced individual wellbeing after demanding work hours. For instance, evidence from social research reveals that approximately 80% of people utilize television for relaxation and leisure time as a direct result of accessible entertainment value provided by diverse cultural activities and entertainment programs.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that television as a companion may also give rise to certain challenges regarding social connections. This is largely because excessive reliance on entertainment programs for companionship are likely to generate feelings of isolation and reduced face-to-face social interactions, which may subsequently cause people experiencing loneliness to substitute real human connections with media consumption. According to research by social studies, over 60% of individuals who depend heavily on television for companionship have been affected by diminished social connections stemming from reduced community involvement and limited relationship building with actual people.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that television indeed serves important functions for relaxation and companionship as the statement suggests, yet the influence of balanced media consumption and maintaining real social connections is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for individuals to dedicate adequate attention and moderation to managing television usage effectively while preserving meaningful human interactions.

## Special Note: "To What Extent" Essays

**When a prompt asks "To what extent do you think the statement is true?":**

**Template B Solution:**
- Intro: "I contend that the statement holds **considerable validity**, yet..."
- Paragraph 2 = Support the statement (show where it's true)
- Paragraph 3 = Acknowledge limitations (show where it has challenges)
- Conclusion = Balanced position ("indeed serves functions... yet balanced usage is important")

**Key phrases for "to what extent":**
- "holds considerable validity" (Section 8)
- "indeed serves important functions as the statement suggests"
- "yet the influence of... is substantial"

Template B allows balanced position without contradicting yourself!
