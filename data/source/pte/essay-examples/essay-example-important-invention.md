# PTE Essay Example - Most Important Invention

## Topic
> There are many important inventions in the modern world, including antibiotics, airplanes and computers. What do you believe is the most important invention of the past hundred years? Why?

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of important inventions and their contributions to the modern world has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that computers represent the most important invention of the past hundred years due to their substantial influence across multiple sectors, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of computers is its potential to facilitate technological progress and economic growth. In particular, computers can foster innovation in educational institutions, business activities, and communication systems, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as improved productivity levels and enhanced global governance. For instance, institutional research reveals that approximately 80% of modern organizations experienced significant development as a direct result of computer technology implementation.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that other inventions such as antibiotics and airplanes may also give rise to considerable validity. This is largely because antibiotics have saved countless lives through medical advancement, while airplanes have revolutionized transportation and cross-border cooperation, which may subsequently cause these inventions to be viewed as equally vital. According to research by development studies, over 60% of global progress stemming from combined technological achievements.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that while antibiotics and airplanes remain crucial, computers represent the most important invention as the vital component of modern civilization, as the influence of digital technology across all sectors is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for societies to dedicate adequate resources to managing technological development effectively.

## Answer Type: "What Do You Believe + Why" Question

### Template B Adaptation for Choice Questions:

**Structure:**
- **Paragraph 1:** State your choice clearly ("I contend that computers...")
- **Paragraph 2:** Why your choice is most important (advantages, impact)
- **Paragraph 3:** Acknowledge other options but explain why yours is better
- **Paragraph 4:** Reaffirm your choice

**For this topic:**
- **Clear choice required**: Must pick ONE (computers, antibiotics, or airplanes)
- **Explain "why"**: Paragraph 2 gives reasons (economic, educational, communication impact)
- **Acknowledge alternatives**: Paragraph 3 respects other inventions but maintains position
- **Reaffirm conclusion**: "computers represent the most important invention"

**This approach:**
- Shows critical thinking (acknowledges other options)
- Maintains clear position throughout
- Provides specific reasons
- Scores well on content and structure

- Reaffirm choice in conclusion

❌ **Don't:**
- Say "all are equally important" (avoids the question)

- Ignore the other options mentioned
- Give vague reasons
