

## Example 12: Work-Life Balance

**Question:** Nowadays, it is increasingly more difficult to maintain the right balance between work and other aspects of one's life, such as time with family and leisure needs. How important do you think this balance is? Why do people find it hard to achieve?

### Fill-in Terms (13 unique)

**Main Topic:** work-life balance (repeat 8+ times)

**Subjects:** people, workers

**Positive Outcomes (Why Important):** mental health, family time, leisure activities, reduced stress

**Negative Outcomes (Why Hard):** long working hours, job demands, financial pressure, career expectations

**Evidence Sources:** workplace research, health studies

**Conclusion:** employers and individuals

### Complete Essay (223 words)

In recent years, the topic of **work-life balance** has emerged as a significant subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that **work-life balance** is **highly important** for **people** despite being difficult to achieve, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **work-life balance** is its potential to bring about **mental health and emotional stability**. In particular, **work-life balance** can facilitate **family time and leisure activities**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **personal happiness and reduced stress**. For instance, evidence from **workplace research** shows that many **workers** experienced **better health and improved satisfaction** as a direct result of **maintaining work-life balance**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **work-life balance** may also give rise to certain challenges. This is largely because **modern work culture** is likely to generate **long working hours and high job demands**, which may subsequently lead to **financial pressure and career expectations**. According to research by **health studies**, a significant number of **people** have been affected by **time constraints and competing priorities** resulting from **difficulty achieving work-life balance**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of **work-life balance**, since its advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany it. Thus, it is essential for **employers and individuals** to allocate sufficient resources to address these challenges effectively.




### Complete Essay (235 words)



The question of **how important work-life balance is** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **maintaining balance between work and personal life** is **extremely important for wellbeing** despite being **difficult to achieve**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.



To begin with, one compelling reason why **work-life balance is essential** lies in its impact on **mental health and physical wellbeing**. In particular, **achieving work-life balance** can facilitate **strong family relationships and personal happiness**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **increased productivity and job satisfaction**. **Employees with healthy work-life balance** demonstrate **better stress management**. For instance, **workers who maintain regular schedules** report significantly **improved mental health and family relationships** compared to those **with excessive work demands**.



However, it is important to acknowledge that **achieving this balance** presents certain challenges in **modern work environments**. This is largely because **competitive work culture and financial obligations** can generate **long working hours and constant connectivity**, which may subsequently lead to **reduced personal time and increased family stress**. **Workers in demanding jobs** who **work long hours** experience pressure to **check work emails after hours**. As a result, many **working professionals** face **work-related stress and difficulty maintaining boundaries** that make it difficult to achieve **healthy work-life separation**.



Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **work-life balance** is critically important for overall wellbeing, though **modern work demands** make it increasingly challenging. Thus, it is essential for **employers and employees** to prioritize strategies that support **better balance and healthier work practices**.
