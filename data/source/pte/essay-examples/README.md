## Template B Structure

**Base Words:** 155 | **Target Range:** 200-300 words (with topic fills)

### Paragraph 1 - Introduction (28 words base)
```
In recent years, the topic of [keywords] has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion.

I contend that [your position], and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.
```

### Paragraph 2 - Advantage/Reason (58 words base)
```
To begin with, one compelling [reason/advantage/feature] of [keywords] is its potential to facilitate [keywords].

In particular, [keywords] can foster [keywords], ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as [keywords].

For instance, evidence from [source 1] reveals that approximately 80% of [relevant subjects] experienced [keywords] as a direct result of [keywords].
```

### Paragraph 3 - Challenge/Counterpoint (51 words base)
```
However, it is equally important to acknowledge that [keywords] may also give rise to certain challenges.

This is largely because [keywords] are likely to generate [keywords], which may subsequently cause [keywords].

According to research by [source 2], over 60% of [relevant subjects] have been affected by [keywords] stemming from [keywords].
```

### Paragraph 4 - Conclusion (43 words base)
```
Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that [rephrase position], as the influence of [keywords] and [keywords] is substantial and far-reaching.

Thus, it is imperative for [relevant subjects] to dedicate adequate [resources/time/efforts/energy/money] to managing this issue effectively.
```

**Why Template B Works:**
- ✅ Universal "However" connector works for ALL essay types (advantage/disadvantage, agree/disagree, to what extent)
- ✅ Balanced structure acknowledges both sides naturally
- ✅ Academic tone with moderate vocabulary built-in
- ✅ Clear placeholders for topic keywords and vocabulary integration

## Common Fill Patterns

### Introduction `[keywords]` Patterns
Combine 2-3 topic words:
- travel in education ← travel + education
- work-life balance ← work + life + balance
- building design effects ← building + design + effects
- tourism development ← tourism + development

### `[your position]` Patterns
Reuse 4-6 topic keywords + 1 moderate word:
- "travel is a **valuable** component of quality education" (5 topic words + "valuable")
- "**maintaining** balance between work and life aspects is **crucial**" (4 topic words + "maintaining" + "crucial")
- "architectural features exert **substantial** influence on residential areas" (3 topic words + "substantial")

### `[source 1/2]` Patterns
Simple, realistic, generic sources:
- university research
- educational institutions
- workplace studies
- organizational research
- academic studies
- policy research
- environmental studies

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Made-up specific sources ("Annual Report of Global Education Board")
- ❌ Overly specific citations ("Johnson et al. 2023")

### `[relevant subjects]` Patterns
Match to topic context:
- **Education topics:** students, educators, educational programs, universities
- **Work topics:** employees, professionals, workers, organizations
- **Society topics:** governments, communities, citizens, policymakers
- **Environment topics:** individuals, nations, environmental agencies
- **Technology topics:** users, developers, technology companies

### Evidence Framing Patterns
Template B provides: "approximately 80%" and "over 60%"
- Use when topic provides statistics
- Replace with qualitative evidence if needed:
  - "many students experienced..."
  - "a significant number of employees have..."

## Special Cases

### Two-Question Essays
**Example:** work-life-balance.md asks "How important?" AND "Why difficult?"

**Solution:** Template B's advantage → challenge structure naturally answers both:
- Paragraph 2 = Answer Q1 (Why important? → advantages of balance)
- Paragraph 3 = Answer Q2 (Why difficult? → challenges/reasons)
- Paragraph 4 = Balanced conclusion addressing both

### Opinion Scale Essays ("To what extent")
**Example:** travel-education-26-26.md asks "To what extent do you agree?"

**Solution:** Template B allows balanced position:
- Intro: "I contend that travel is **valuable** even though scholars succeed without it"
- Para 2: Advantages of travel (support your claim)
- Para 3: However, home study also has merit (acknowledge counter)
- Conclusion: Both have value, but X adds unique benefits (balanced position)

### Choice Essays ("Which is most important?")
**Example:** important-invention.md asks to choose among antibiotics/airplanes/computers

**Solution:** Template B works with clear choice:
- Intro: "I contend that **antibiotics** are most important"
- Para 2: Advantages of antibiotics (why it's #1)
- Para 3: However, other inventions also contribute (acknowledge others)
- Conclusion: Antibiotics have greatest impact (reaffirm choice)
