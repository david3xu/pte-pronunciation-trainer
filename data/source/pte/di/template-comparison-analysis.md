## How to Speak "THAT" - Critical for Fluency! 🎯

### **Rule: "That" ALWAYS goes WITH the phrase BEFORE it**

### ✅ Correct Pronunciation Flow

| Linking Phrase | Pause Point | Content |
|----------------|-------------|---------|
| **it is clear that** ⏸️ | [pause here] | China has the highest population |
| **it is evident that** ⏸️ | [pause here] | technology reached 9 million |
| **another point is that** ⏸️ | [pause here] | business has grown rapidly |

### ❌ Wrong - Don't Do This!

```
"it is clear" [pause] "that China has..."  ← WRONG! Breaks flow
```

### ✅ Right - Do This!

```
"it is clear that" [pause] "China has..."  ← CORRECT! Smooth flow
```

---

## Why This Matters for Fluency

**When you say:** "it is clear that"
- Your brain prepares to describe WHAT is clear
- The "that" signals: "here comes the content!"
- Natural pause AFTER "that" gives you time to think

**Think of it like:**
- "it is clear that" = **The Introduction**
- [micro pause]
- "China has the highest..." = **The Content**

## Phrases WITH "that" vs WITHOUT "that"

### **Phrases with "that"** (speak together as one unit)
- it is clear **that** ⏸️ [content]
- it is evident **that** ⏸️ [content]
- another point is **that** ⏸️ [content]
- we can see **that** ⏸️ [content]
- we also see **that** ⏸️ [content]

### **Phrases WITHOUT "that"** (pause immediately after)
- however ⏸️ [content]
- on the other hand ⏸️ [content]
- firstly ⏸️ [content]
- conversely ⏸️ [content]

## 🎯 Recommended Combination (Easiest to Speak)

| Point | Phrase | Pronunciation |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | **it is clear that** | "it-is-clear-that" [pause] content |
| 2 | **however** | "however" [pause] content |
| 3 | **on the other hand** | "on-the-other-hand" [pause] content |

**Why this combo works:**
- Point 1 has "that" → natural bridge to your first observation
- Point 2 is single word → quick contrast marker
- Point 3 is phrase without "that" → smooth addition
