# PTE Describe Image - Just Linking Words (No Template)

## Strategy: Natural Speaking with Linking Words

**Goal:** Speak naturally for ~30 seconds using linking words to connect your 3 observations

**NO TEMPLATE** - Just describe what you see with smooth transitions!

---

## **The 10 Easiest Linking Words** (Pick 3 per image)

| Position | Linking Word | Usage |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| **Start** | First / Firstly | First observation |
| **Start** | We can see | First observation |
| **Start** | Looking at this | First observation |
| **Middle** | Also / Next | Second observation |
| **Middle** | However | Contrast/different point |
| **Middle** | Additionally | Add more info |
| **End** | Finally / Lastly | Third observation |
| **End** | Moreover | Additional point |
| **End** | What's more | Additional point |
| **Wrap** | Overall / In summary | Quick conclusion |

---

## **30-Second Formula (No Template)**

### **Time Breakdown:**
- **Intro (5 secs):** Say what the image is
- **Point 1 (8 secs):** First observation + details
- **Point 2 (8 secs):** Second observation + details
- **Point 3 (6 secs):** Third observation
- **Wrap (3 secs):** Quick summary

**Total: ~30 seconds**

---

## **Example 1: Kitchen Image**

### **Natural Speaking (28 seconds):**

```
Looking at this picture, I can see a kitchen restaurant on the street.

First, there's a large sign that says "kitchen" on the white building.

Also, I notice several tables and chairs placed outside for outdoor dining.

Finally, there's one person sitting inside near the window.

Overall, this shows a small street cafe.
```

**Word count:** ~48 words ≈ 28 seconds at normal speed

---

## **Example 2: Same Image, Different Links**

```
This image shows a restaurant called kitchen.

We can see, the building is white with glass windows and doors.

Additionally, outdoor furniture is set up on the sidewalk.

What's more, a customer is visible inside the restaurant.

In summary, this is a modern street eatery.
```

**Word count:** ~45 words ≈ 27 seconds

## **Example 3: Bar Chart (Natural, No Template)**

```
This bar chart gives information about student enrollment.

Finally, the UK has the smallest number at roughly 100 million.

Overall, China dominates in student population.
```

## **How to Hit 30 Seconds Every Time**

### **Formula:**

1. **Intro:** 1 sentence describing the image (5-7 words)
2. **Link 1 + Point 1:** Linking word + observation + detail (12-15 words)
3. **Link 2 + Point 2:** Linking word + observation + detail (12-15 words)
4. **Link 3 + Point 3:** Linking word + observation (8-10 words)
5. **Wrap:** Quick summary (5-7 words)

**Total:** 45-50 words = 27-30 seconds

- **Finally** → Point 3

### **Combo 2** (Natural)
- **We can see** → Point 1

### **Combo 3** (With Contrast)
- **First** → Point 1
- **However** → Point 2 (when contrasting)
- **Finally** → Point 3

## **Practice Rule:**

🎯 **3 observations + 3 linking words + details = 30 seconds**

**Simple math:**
- Each observation with details = ~10 seconds
- 3 observations = 30 seconds
- Add intro (5s) + wrap (3s) = 38 seconds
- Adjust by speaking slightly faster

## **Your Kitchen Image - 3 Different Ways**

### **Way 1: Simple**
```
This shows a kitchen restaurant.
First, the sign is on top.
Also, chairs are outside.
Finally, someone sits inside.
Overall, a street cafe.
```
**Words:** 24 ≈ 18 seconds (TOO SHORT)

### **Way 2: With Details** ✅
```
This picture shows a kitchen restaurant on a street corner.
First, a large "kitchen" sign is mounted on the white building.
Also, multiple tables and chairs are arranged outside for customers.
Finally, I can see one person sitting inside near the glass window.
Overall, this represents a casual dining establishment.
```
**Words:** 53 ≈ 32 seconds (PERFECT!)

### **Way 3: Maximum Details**
```
Looking at this image, we can see a restaurant named "kitchen" located on a street.
First, the main building is painted white with a prominent sign displaying the word "kitchen" across the top.
Additionally, there are several pieces of outdoor furniture including tables and chairs positioned on the sidewalk for al fresco dining.
What's more, through the large glass windows, a single customer is visible sitting inside the establishment.
In summary, this image depicts a contemporary urban cafe with both indoor and outdoor seating options.
```
**Words:** 87 ≈ 52 seconds (TOO LONG)

## **Quick Checklist:**

Before you speak, ask:
1. ✅ Do I have 3 clear points?
2. ✅ Did I pick my 3 linking words?
3. ✅ Did I add details to each point? (not just "sign" but "large kitchen sign on white building")
4. ✅ Is my intro + wrap included?

If yes to all → You'll hit 30 seconds! 🎯
