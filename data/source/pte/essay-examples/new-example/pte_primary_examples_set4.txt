## Example 19: Celebrity Privacy

### **Question:**
> "People who are famous entertainers or sportspeople should give up the right to privacy because this is the price of fame. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this point of view?"

### **Question Type:** Agree/Disagree

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 37):**

**Main topic:** celebrity privacy rights  
**Paraphrases:** privacy for famous people, celebrity privacy, privacy rights of celebrities, famous people's privacy

**Subjects:** celebrities, fans

**Positive outcomes (Why privacy needed):** mental health and personal wellbeing, family protection and safe environment, personal freedom and basic rights

**Negative outcomes (Why privacy lost):** media attention and public interest, social media and constant exposure, fan expectations and public demand

**Idiom:** "in the spotlight" (under public attention)

**Stakeholders:** celebrities and media organizations

---

### **Complete Essay (234 words)**

The question of **whether famous people should sacrifice privacy as a cost of fame** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I disagree, contending that **celebrity privacy rights deserve protection** for **celebrities** despite **their public status**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling reason why **privacy for famous people** should be protected is its importance for **mental health and personal wellbeing**. In particular, **celebrity privacy** can facilitate **family protection and safe environments**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **personal freedom and basic human rights**. **Celebrities who maintain privacy boundaries** demonstrate **better mental health and family stability**. For instance, **famous individuals who protect their private lives** report significantly **reduced stress and better relationships** compared to those **who face constant media intrusion**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **privacy rights of celebrities** face certain challenges in **media-driven societies**. This is largely because **fame and public status** create **intense media attention and public interest**, which then lead to **constant social media exposure and fan demands**. **Many famous people** find themselves **in the spotlight constantly**, which creates **pressure to share personal details and difficulty maintaining boundaries**. As a result, many **celebrities** have **difficulty protecting their families** that make it difficult to **maintain normal private lives**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **celebrity privacy rights** should be respected, since basic human rights remain important despite public status. Thus, it is essential for **celebrities and media organizations** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 20: Parental Legal Responsibility

### **Question:**
> "Should parents be held legally responsible for the actions of their children? Do you agree with this opinion? Support your position with your own study, experience or observations."

### **Question Type:** Agree/Disagree

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 31):**

**Main topic:** parental legal responsibility  
**Paraphrases:** holding parents accountable, parental accountability, parents' legal obligations, legal responsibility for children

**Subjects:** parents, children

**Positive outcomes:** stronger supervision and better guidance, personal responsibility and family values, reduced youth crime and safer communities

**Negative outcomes:** unfair legal burden and financial stress, family conflict and damaged relationships, limited parental control and external influences

**Idiom:** "keep tabs on" (monitor closely)

**Stakeholders:** parents and schools

---

### **Complete Essay (237 words)**

The question of **whether parents should be held legally accountable for their children's actions** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **parental legal responsibility offers substantial benefits** for **society** despite **certain limitations**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **holding parents accountable** is its potential to bring about **stronger supervision and better guidance**. In particular, **parental accountability** can facilitate **personal responsibility and family values**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **reduced youth crime and safer communities**. **Parents with legal obligations** demonstrate **more active involvement in children's lives**. For instance, **families where parents accept responsibility** report significantly **better youth behavior and reduced delinquency** compared to those **without parental accountability measures**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **parents' legal obligations** face certain challenges in **complex social environments**. This is largely because **external influences and peer pressure** create **situations beyond parental control**, which then lead to **unfair legal burden and family stress**. **Many parents** struggle to **keep tabs on their children's activities constantly**, which creates **difficulty monitoring all behaviors and preventing all mistakes**. As a result, many **families** have **strained relationships due to legal pressure** that make it difficult to **maintain supportive family dynamics**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of **parental legal responsibility**, since its advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany it. Thus, it is essential for **parents and schools** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 21: Medical Technology and Life Expectancy

### **Question:**
> "Medical technology is responsible for increasing the average life expectancy. Do you think it is a curse or a blessing?"

### **Question Type:** Opinion (Evaluate as positive or negative)

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 30):**

**Main topic:** increased life expectancy  
**Paraphrases:** longer lifespans, extended life, greater longevity, living longer

**Subjects:** people, society

**Positive outcomes:** family time and life experience, social contributions and stronger bonds, improved quality of life

**Negative outcomes:** chronic illness and long-term care, ageing population and healthcare costs, financial pressure on systems

**Idiom:** "blessing in disguise" - BUT complex, so use: "golden years" (retirement/later life)

**Stakeholders:** governments and healthcare systems

---

### **Complete Essay (233 words)**

The question of **whether medical technology extending lifespans is beneficial or harmful** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **increased life expectancy represents a blessing** for **people** despite **certain challenges**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **increased life expectancy** is its potential to bring about **extended family time and valuable life experience**. In particular, **longer lifespans** can facilitate **continued social contributions and stronger family bonds**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **improved quality of life and intergenerational wisdom sharing**. **People who live longer** demonstrate **greater life satisfaction and family connection**. For instance, **individuals enjoying their golden years** report significantly **richer family relationships and continued purpose** compared to **previous generations with shorter lifespans**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **extended life** faces certain challenges in **modern healthcare systems**. This is largely because **longer lifespans** create **increased chronic illness and extended care needs**, which then lead to **ageing populations and rising healthcare costs**. **Many societies** experience **pressure from supporting growing elderly populations**, which creates **financial strain on healthcare systems and pension programs**. As a result, many **governments** have **difficulty providing adequate care** that make it difficult to **sustain quality healthcare for all**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **increased life expectancy** is predominantly positive, since its human benefits remain significant despite financial challenges. Thus, it is essential for **governments and healthcare systems** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 22: Studying Classic Plays

### **Question:**
> "There are both problems and benefits for high school students to study plays and works of theatres written centuries ago. Discuss and use your own experience."

### **Question Type:** Opinion/Evaluation (Acknowledges both sides)

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 28):**

**Main topic:** studying classic plays  
**Paraphrases:** learning classical drama, reading old plays, classic literature study, historical theater study

**Subjects:** students, learners

**Positive outcomes:** cultural awareness and historical understanding, enhanced critical thinking and deeper knowledge, language skills and literary appreciation

**Negative outcomes:** difficult language and outdated themes, low student interest and boring content, limited relevance and modern disconnect

**Idiom:** "behind the times" (outdated)

**Stakeholders:** schools and educators

---

### **Complete Essay (236 words)**

The question of **whether high school students benefit from learning classical drama** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **studying classic plays offers substantial benefits** for **students** despite **certain limitations**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **studying classic plays** is its potential to bring about **cultural awareness and historical understanding**. In particular, **learning classical drama** can facilitate **enhanced critical thinking and deeper literary knowledge**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **improved language skills and literary appreciation**. **Students who engage with classic theater** demonstrate **stronger analytical abilities and cultural literacy**. For instance, **learners who study Shakespeare and classical works** report significantly **improved understanding of human nature and literary techniques** compared to those **who study only contemporary literature**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **reading old plays** faces certain challenges in **modern classrooms**. This is largely because **archaic language and historical context** create **difficult comprehension and low initial interest**, which then lead to **student disengagement and limited perceived relevance**. **Many students** find **classic works behind the times**, which creates **resistance to studying historical literature and preference for modern texts**. As a result, many **young learners** have **difficulty connecting with classical themes** that make it difficult to **appreciate the lasting value of these works**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of **studying classic plays**, since its advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany it. Thus, it is essential for **schools and educators** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 23: Time for Personal Life

### **Question:**
> "The time people devote to jobs leaves very little time for personal life. How widespread is the problem? What problem will this shortage of time cause?"

### **Question Type:** How Widespread + What Problems (Two-part question)

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 35):**

**Main topic:** shortage of personal time  
**Paraphrases:** limited personal time, lack of free time, insufficient personal time, time scarcity

**Subjects:** workers, employees

**Positive outcomes (Evidence of widespread):** long working hours and job demands, modern work culture and career pressure, global workplace trends

**Negative outcomes (Problems caused):** health issues and chronic stress, family problems and relationship difficulties, reduced happiness and lower motivation

**Idiom:** "pressed for time" (rushed, lacking time)

**Stakeholders:** employers and policymakers

---

### **Complete Essay (237 words)**

The question of **how widespread the problem of limited personal time is and what issues it creates** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **shortage of personal time** is **extremely widespread globally** and creates **serious problems** for **workers**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, compelling evidence shows that **limited personal time** has become widespread due to **long working hours and increasing job demands**. In particular, **modern work culture** generates **career pressure and expectation of constant availability**, ultimately leading to broader patterns such as **global workplace trends toward longer hours**. **Employees across industries** demonstrate **significantly reduced personal time**. For instance, **workers in competitive sectors** experience **substantially less free time** compared to **previous generations with clearer work-life boundaries**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge the serious problems that **insufficient personal time** creates in **modern life**. This is largely because **lack of free time** generates **chronic stress and health issues**, which then lead to **family problems and relationship difficulties**. **Many employees** feel **constantly pressed for time**, which creates **reduced happiness and declining motivation at work**. As a result, many **workers** experience **mental exhaustion and deteriorating personal relationships** that make it difficult to **maintain overall wellbeing**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **shortage of personal time** is both highly widespread and seriously problematic. Thus, it is essential for **employers and policymakers** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 24: Mass Communication Impact

### **Question:**
> "The information revolution by modern mass communication has both positive and negative consequences for individuals and for society. To what extent do you agree?"

### **Question Type:** Advantages and Disadvantages

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 25):**

**Main topic:** modern mass communication  
**Paraphrases:** mass communication revolution, information revolution, modern communication, digital communication

**Subjects:** individuals, society

**Positive outcomes:** improved communication and global connectivity, faster information access and knowledge sharing, online learning and better education

**Negative outcomes:** excessive screen time and digital addiction, social isolation and physical health issues, misinformation and privacy concerns

**Idiom:** "keep in touch" (maintain contact)

**Stakeholders:** society and individuals

---

### **Complete Essay (238 words)**

The question of **whether the information revolution brings both benefits and drawbacks** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I agree, contending that **modern mass communication offers substantial benefits** for **individuals and society** while also creating **certain limitations**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **modern mass communication** is its potential to bring about **improved global connectivity and instant communication**. In particular, **mass communication revolution** can facilitate **faster information access and widespread knowledge sharing**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **online learning opportunities and better education access**. **People using modern communication tools** demonstrate **better information access and social connections**. For instance, **individuals who use digital platforms to keep in touch** report significantly **improved ability to maintain relationships** compared to those **relying only on traditional communication methods**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **information revolution** faces certain challenges in **digital societies**. This is largely because **constant connectivity** creates **excessive screen time and digital addiction**, which then lead to **social isolation and declining physical health**. **Many individuals** experience **pressure from continuous digital communication**, which creates **misinformation exposure and privacy concerns**. As a result, many **people** have **difficulty managing information overload** that make it difficult to **maintain healthy digital habits**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **modern mass communication** has both significant advantages and disadvantages for individuals and society. Thus, it is essential for **society and individuals** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.
