## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of experiential learning in formal education system has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that experiential learning is beneficial in high school and college when balanced with traditional teaching methods, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of experiential learning is its potential to facilitate practical skills development and knowledge retention. In particular, learning by doing can foster active engagement and hands-on experience in educational institutions, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as improved student performance and enhanced learning outcomes. For instance, educational research reveals that approximately 80% of students in experiential programs experienced better academic excellence as a direct result of practical application.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that traditional teaching methods may also give rise to considerable validity. This is largely because systematic instruction and classroom instruction are likely to generate solid foundational knowledge and academic discipline, which may subsequently cause traditional form of teaching to remain effective in high schools and colleges. According to research by educational institutions, over 60% of students achieved learning outcomes through teacher-led instruction stemming from structured curriculum design.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that experiential learning is beneficial in high schools and colleges as a vital component, as the influence of both hands-on experience and traditional approaches is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for educational institutions to dedicate adequate resources to managing balanced teaching approaches effectively.

## Comparison Question Strategy

**When comparing two approaches (A vs B):**

✅ **Introduction**: State preference + acknowledge both have value
✅ **Paragraph 2**: Why your choice is beneficial (3-4 reasons)
✅ **Paragraph 3**: Why alternative also has value (shows critical thinking)
✅ **Conclusion**: Reaffirm your choice while respecting alternative

**For "experiential vs traditional":**
- Choose experiential as primary → Para 2
- Acknowledge traditional value → Para 3
- Conclude: Best approach uses both, with experiential as vital component

**This is better than:**
- ❌ Saying "both are equally important" (avoids taking position)
- ❌ Completely dismissing one approach (too absolute)
- ❌ Changing position mid-essay (inconsistent)
