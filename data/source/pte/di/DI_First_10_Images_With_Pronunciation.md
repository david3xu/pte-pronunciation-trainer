## 🔥 MOST CRITICAL PRONUNCIATION RULES

### Rule 1: Compound Numbers
- ✅ "SEVENTY-two" (stress first part)
- ❌ "seventy two" (flat, no stress)

### Rule 2: Years
- ✅ "NINETEEN ninety-five" (two stresses)
- ❌ "one nine nine five" (wrong format)

### Rule 3: Thousands
- ✅ "THREE thousand FIVE hundred" (stress both)
- ❌ "three-thousand-five-hundred" (run together)

### Rule 4: Academic Words
- ✅ "DEM-on-strates" (stress first)
- ❌ "dem-ON-strates" (stress second - wrong!)

### Rule 5: Analysis
- ✅ "uh-NAL-ih-sis" (stress second)
- ❌ "ann-AL-isis" (wrong vowels!)
