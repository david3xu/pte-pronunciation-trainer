# PTE Essay Example - Television Functions

## Topic
> Television serves many useful functions. It helps people to relax. Besides, it can also be seen as a companion for the lonely. To what extent do you agree with this? Explain why with your own experience?

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 250** | **Template: B** | **Method: Vocabulary File Only**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of television and its useful functions has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that television serves valuable purposes in helping people relax and providing companionship for the lonely, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of television is its potential to facilitate mental health wellbeing and overall life satisfaction. In particular, television can foster relaxation through entertainment and stress reduction, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as improved quality of life and better individual wellbeing. For instance, social research reveals that approximately 80% of individuals who regularly watch television experienced enhanced relaxation as a direct result of engaging entertainment programs.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that television as a companion for lonely people may also give rise to significant validity. This is largely because television provides social connections through content and reduces feelings of isolation, which may subsequently cause lonely individuals to gain emotional support and community involvement through programs. According to research by social behavior studies, over 60% of people living alone have found companionship through television stemming from regular viewing of favorite programs.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that television indeed serves useful functions for both relaxation and companionship, as the influence of entertainment value and social connection is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for individuals to dedicate appropriate time to managing television use effectively while recognizing its positive contributions.
