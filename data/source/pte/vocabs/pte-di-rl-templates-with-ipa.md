## Pronunciation Notes

**Key Stress Patterns:**
- All CAPITALIZED words should be stressed (louder/clearer)
- Numbers: Always stress FIRST part (SEVENTY-two, THREE hundred)
- Pauses: Use "|" marks as 0.3-0.5 second pause points
- Transitions: "Following THAT", "SUBSEQUENTLY", "Additionally"
- Conclusions: "In CONCLUSION", "OVERALL", "In SUMMARY"

**Common Difficult Words:**
- **approximately** = "uh-PRAHK-sih-muht-lee" (5 syllables, stress 2nd)
- **DEMONSTRATES** = "DEM-uhn-strayts" (3 syllables, stress 1st)
- **COMPARATIVE** = "kuhm-PAIR-uh-tiv" (4 syllables, stress 2nd)
- **GEOGRAPHICAL** = "jee-uh-GRAF-ih-kuhl" (5 syllables, stress 3rd)
- **COMPREHENSIVELY** = "kahm-prih-HEN-siv-lee" (5 syllables, stress 3rd)
