# PTE Describe Image - Linking Phrases Reference

## ALL Linking Phrases from Nakul's Video

### **For Introduction**
- The image represents
- The image is
- This gives information about

### **For X-axis/Y-axis (if present)**
- The x-axis talks about
- The y-axis talks about

---

## **For Point 1** (First Key Content)

1. **it is evident that**
2. **it is clear that**
3. **firstly**

**Example:**
- "It is evident that in the year 2019 students enrolled in business is very low"
- "Firstly in the month of January the temperature is around 18°C"
- "First it is clear that the first prime minister of India is Jawaharlal Nehru"

---

## **For Point 2** (Second Key Content - Contrast/Comparison)

4. **however**
5. **on the other hand**
6. **second highest is**
7. **the second is**

**Example:**
- "However students enrolled in technology is very high"
- "However in the month of June and July the temperature has hit the highest"
- "Second highest is other category with a value of 29%"
- "The second is Gulzarilal Nanda"

---

## **For Point 3** (Third Key Content - Additional Point)

8. **conversely**
9. **another interesting point is that**
10. **the third highest is**
11. **the third is**
12. **the next is**

**Example:**
- "Another interesting point is that" (mentioned in structure)
- "The third highest is a Marathi language with a value of only 9%"
- "The third is Lal Bahadur Shastri"
- "The next is Tamil Telugu and Bengali"

---

## **For Additional Points** (If time permits)

13. **respectively**
14. **we can see**

**Example:**
- "The next is Tamil Telugu and Bengali with a value of 6 7 and 8% respectively"
- "In the year 2022 we can see a spike in the students enrolled for business"

---

## **For Conclusion**

15. **in conclusion**
16. **as a result**

**Example:**
- "In conclusion" (mentioned in structure)
- "As a result" (mentioned as alternative)

---

## **Top 3 EASIEST Combinations** (Recommended)

### **Option 1** (Most Used by Nakul)
1. **it is clear that** (Point 1)

3. **on the other hand** (Point 3)

### **Option 2** (Variation)
1. **it is evident that** (Point 1)
2. **conversely** (Point 2)

### **Option 3** (Simple)
1. **firstly** (Point 1)
2. **secondly** / **the second highest is** (Point 2)
