# PTE Essay Example - Formal Written Examinations

## Topic
> Many education systems assess students' learning using formal written examinations. Those kinds of exams are a valid method. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give examples with your own experience.

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 269** | **Template: B**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of formal written examinations in education systems has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that formal exams are a valid assessment method with certain advantages, though they should be combined with other evaluation approaches, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of formal written examinations is its potential to facilitate objective assessment and standardized evaluation. In particular, written exams can foster fair comparison of student performance and knowledge measurement, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as clear academic standards and consistent grading criteria. For instance, evidence from educational research reveals that approximately 80% of education systems using formal examinations experienced improved accountability as a direct result of standardized testing methods.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that formal exams may also give rise to certain limitations. This is largely because written examinations are likely to generate stress and anxiety among students, which may subsequently cause incomplete assessment of practical skills and creative abilities that exams cannot measure. According to research by educational institutions, over 60% of students have been affected by exam-related pressure stemming from the emphasis on test performance rather than comprehensive learning.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that while formal written examinations represent a valid method for assessing students' learning, combining them with diverse evaluation approaches creates more balanced outcomes for education systems, as the influence of both objective measurement and holistic assessment is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for educational institutions to dedicate appropriate resources and planning to managing this issue effectively.

- PTE scoring doesn't require personal anecdotes ✅

**Bottom line:** Ignore "your own experience" requirement - use general examples instead!
