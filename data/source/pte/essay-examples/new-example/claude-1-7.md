## Example 1: Work-Life Balance ✅

### Question
**"Nowadays, it is increasingly more difficult to maintain the right balance between work and other aspects of one's life, such as time with family and leisure needs. How important do you think this balance is? Why do people find it hard to achieve?"**

### Question Type
How Important / Why Hard (Two-part question)

### Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 12)
- **Main topic:** work-life balance
- **Paraphrases:** maintaining this equilibrium, achieving this balance, a balanced lifestyle
- **Subjects:** people, employees, workers
- **Why Important:** improved mental health and emotional stability, family time and leisure activities, personal happiness and reduced stress
- **Why Hard:** high career expectations and competitive pressure, long working hours and excessive job demands, time constraints and competing priorities
- **Idiom:** "around the clock" (24/7, constantly)
- **Stakeholders:** employers and individuals

### Complete Essay (237 words)

The question of **how important it is to balance work with personal life** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **maintaining this equilibrium** is **critically important** for **people** despite **being difficult to achieve**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling reason why **work-life balance** is essential lies in its impact on **improved mental health and emotional stability**. In particular, **achieving this balance** can facilitate **family time and leisure activities**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **personal happiness and reduced stress**. **Employees who protect personal boundaries** demonstrate **better psychological well-being**. For instance, **workers who maintain clear separation between work and home** report significantly **improved mental health and stronger family relationships** compared to those **without clear work-life boundaries**.

However, it is important to acknowledge that **achieving this balance** presents certain challenges in **modern work environments**. This is largely because **competitive work culture** can generate **high career expectations and constant pressure**, which may subsequently lead to **long working hours and excessive job demands**. **Many workplaces** require **around-the-clock availability**, creating pressure to **respond to emails after hours and accept overtime**. As a result, many **employees** face **time constraints and competing priorities** that make it difficult to maintain **a balanced lifestyle**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **work-life balance** is critically important for overall well-being, though **modern work demands** make it increasingly challenging. Thus, it is essential for **employers and individuals** to prioritize strategies that support **healthy work-life boundaries** effectively.

### Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 8)

- **Paraphrases:** grade deductions for late work, penalty systems, deadline enforcement
- **Subjects:** students, universities, learners
- **Positive outcomes:** time management, planning skills, personal responsibility, work discipline
- **Challenges:** unfair situations, student stress, emergency circumstances
- **Idiom:** "make the grade" (meet required standards)

### Complete Essay (237 words)

The question of **whether universities should implement grade deductions for late submissions** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **late assignment penalties offer substantial benefits** for **students** despite **needing some flexibility**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **late assignment penalties** is their potential to bring about **time management and planning skills**. In particular, **grade deductions for late work** can facilitate **personal responsibility and deadline awareness**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **meeting deadlines and work discipline**. **Students facing clear deadline requirements** demonstrate **better organization skills**. For instance, **learners who must submit work on time to make the grade** report significantly **improved time management and better planning** compared to those **without penalty systems**.

However, it is important to acknowledge that **penalty systems** present certain challenges in **academic environments**. Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that **late assignment penalties** serve important purposes when implemented with appropriate flexibility. Thus, it is essential for **universities and instructors** to implement **these balanced recommendations** effectively.
