# PTE Essay - Quick Fill Guide (Template B)

> **Purpose:** Simple placeholder filling tables for common PTE essay topics
>
> **Rule:** Keep fills SHORT (1-3 words max per placeholder)
>
> **Strategy:** 60-70% topic keywords + 20-30% simple phrases + 10-20% moderate vocabulary

---

## How to Use This Guide

1. Find your topic or similar topic
2. Extract 5-10 keywords from the prompt
3. Use the fill table as a reference
4. **Keep it simple** - don't overcomplicate!
