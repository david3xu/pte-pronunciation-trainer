# PTE Essay Example - Mark Deduction for Late Work

## Topic
> Some universities deduct marks from students' work if it is handed in late. What is your opinion? What other actions do you recommend?

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 279** | **Template: B**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of mark deduction for late work in universities has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that deducting marks can be an effective approach to encourage timely submission, though other supportive actions should also be considered, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of mark deduction is its potential to facilitate academic discipline and time management skills. In particular, implementing penalties for late work can foster student responsibility and respect for deadlines, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as improved work habits and better preparation for professional environments. For instance, evidence from university studies reveals that approximately 80% of students with clear deadline policies experienced enhanced submission punctuality as a direct result of mark deduction systems.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that other actions may also give rise to more effective solutions. This is largely because supportive measures such as deadline extensions for valid reasons and early submission incentives are likely to generate positive motivation rather than punishment-based compliance, which may subsequently cause students to develop better planning skills voluntarily. According to research by educational institutions, over 60% of universities have been able to achieve improved submission rates through alternative approaches stemming from flexible policies and student support services.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that while mark deduction has merit as a disciplinary tool, combining it with supportive actions creates more balanced outcomes for universities and students, as the influence of both accountability and flexibility is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for academic institutions to dedicate appropriate attention and resources to managing this issue effectively.
