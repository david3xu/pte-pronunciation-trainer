# PTE Essay Questions Extracted

## 极高频 极限预测 (Extremely High Frequency - Ultimate Prediction)

### 1. Employee Participation in Decision-Making (#101004)
**Question:** In some companies, some employers involve employees in the decision-making process of products and services. What are the advantages and disadvantages of such a policy?

### 2. Written Examinations (#101005)
**Question:** The formal written examination can be a valid method to assess students' learning. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Use your own experience.

### 3. Travel and Education (#101006)
**Question:** "The value of travel has been overrated". It is said that one can be a brilliant scholar without leaving one's home base. Do you think travel is a necessary component of a quality education?

**Variant:** It is often argued that studying overseas is overrated. There are many scholars who study locally. To what extent do you agree with this?

### 4. Climate Change Responsibility (#101012)
**Question:** Climate change is a concerning global issue. Who should take the responsibilities: governments, big companies or individuals?

### 5. Television Functions (Learning, Relaxation, Companionship) (#101013)
**Question:** Television serves many useful functions. It helps people to relax. We can learn from television, and it can also be seen as a companion for the lonely. To what extent do you agree with this? Explain why with your own experience.

### 6. Experiential Learning (#101040)
**Question:** Some people point out that experiential learning (i.e. learning by doing) can work well in formal education. However, others think a traditional form of teaching is the best. Do you think experiential learning is beneficial in high school or college?

### 7. Deducting Marks for Late Assignments (#101043)
**Question:** Some universities deduct students' marks if assignments are given late. What is your opinion and give your recommendations?

### 8. Combining Work and Study (#101044)
**Question:** In order to study effectively, it requires comfort, peace and time. So it is impossible for a student to combine learning and employment at the same time, because one  distracts the other. Is it realistic to combine them at the same time in our life today? Support your opinion with examples.

### 9. Most Pressing Problem for Governments (#101055)
**Question:** The world's governments and organizations are facing a lot of issues. Which do you think is the most pressing problem for the inhabitants on our planet? And give solutions.

### 10. Building Design Impact (#101056)
**Question:** How does the design of buildings affect, either positively or negatively, where people work and live?

### 11. Work-Life Balance (Importance and Causes) (#101127)
**Question:** Nowadays, it is increasingly more difficult to maintain the right balance between work and other aspects of one's life, such as time with family and leisure needs. How important do you think this balance is? Why do people find it hard to achieve?

### 12. New Invention and Its Impact (#101128)
**Question:** In our technological world, the number of new inventions has been evolving on a daily basis. Please describe a new invention and explain the impact, either beneficial or detrimental, that you feel it has had on society.

### 13. Laws Changing Human Behavior (#101003)
**Question:** Some people think human behaviors can be changed by laws, while others think laws have little effect. What is your opinion.

### 14. Tourism in Less Developed Countries (#101007)
**Question:** For a less developed country, the disadvantages of tourism are as great as the advantages. Please discuss this statement, and give and explain your opinion.

### 15. Information Revolution by Mass Media (#101009)
**Question:** "The information revolution by modern mass communication has both positive and negative consequences for individuals and for society." To what extent do you agree? Explain with your own experience.

### 16. Mass Media Influencing Society (#101010)
**Question:** The mass media, including TV, radio and newspapers, influences our society and shapes our opinions and characters. What is your opinion?

### 17. Mass Media on Younger Generation (#101011)
**Question:** Mass media, including TV and newspapers, have a great influence on humans, particularly on the younger generation. It has a pivotal role in shaping people's opinions. Discuss the extent you agree or disagree. Use your own experience or examples.

### 18. Marketing Consumer Goods (#101015)
**Question:** Should marketing in companies producing consumer goods like food and clothing, place emphasis on the reputation of the company or short-term strategies like discounts and special offers? Why?

### 19. Climate Change Research (#101016)
**Question:** You are given climate change as the field of study. Which area would you prefer? Explain why you pick this particular area of your study and give an example in the area you pick.

### 20. Studying Old Plays (#101028)
**Question:** There are both problems and benefits for high school students to study plays and works of theatres written centuries ago. Discuss and use your own experience.

### 21. Large Shopping Malls vs Small Shops (#101030)
**Question:** In many towns and cities, large shopping malls are replacing small local shops. Do you think this is a positive development? Give your reasons and examples.

### 22. Medical Technology and Life Expectancy (#101039)
**Question:** Medical technology is responsible for increasing the average life expectancy. Do you think it is a curse or a blessing?

### 23. Parental Legal Responsibility (#101042)
**Question:** Should parents be held legally responsible for the actions of their children? Do you agree with this opinion? Support your position with your own study, experience or observations.

### 24. City Life vs Countryside Life (#101062)
**Question:** Living in the countryside or having a city life, which one do you prefer? Please use examples or your personal experience to support your opinion.

### 25. Experience vs Books and Formal Education (#101122)
**Question:** Some people argue that experience is the best teacher. Life experiences can teach more effectively than books or formal school education. How far do you agree with this idea? Support your opinion with reasons and/or your personal experience.

### 26. Digital Media vs Textbooks (#101123)
**Question:** With the increase of digital media available online, the role of the library has become obsolete. Universities should only procure new digital media rather than constantly update textbooks. Discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this position and give your own point of view.

### 27. Public Transport vs Roads (#101124)
**Question:** As cities are expanding, some people claim governments should look forward to creating better networks of public transport available for everyone rather than building more roads for vehicle owning population. What's your opinion? Give some examples or experience to support.

### 28. Work-Life Balance (Prevalence and Problems) (#101126)

### 29. Reducing Working Hours (#101132)

### 30. Celebrities and Privacy (#101134)

### 31. Future Working Hours (#101135)

### 32. Age Restrictions (#101136)

### 33. Foreign Language as Compulsory (#101137)

### 34. Restoring Old Buildings (#101138)

### 35. Children in 21st Century (#101139)

### 36. Maximum Wage (#101140)
