## Key Pronunciation Notes

### ✅ **New Easy Phrases (Fluency-Optimized):**
- **around** = "uh-ROWND" (NOT "approximately")
- **about** = "uh-BOWT" (natural quantifier)
- **On the other END** = "ahn thee UH-thur END" (NOT "Conversely")
- **Worth noting** = "WURTH NOH-ting" (NOT "Additionally")
- **Looking at** = "LOOK-ing AT" (natural transition)
- **Switching to** = "SWICH-ing TOO" (category change)
- **LEADS this group** = "LEEDZ this GROOP" (easier than "demonstrates")

### ❌ **Removed Complex Phrases (Too Formal):**
- ~~approximately~~ → use "around" or "about"
- ~~Conversely~~ → use "On the other END"
- ~~Additionally~~ → use "Worth noting"
- ~~DEMONSTRATES the HIGHEST~~ → use "SHOWS the HIGHEST"
- ~~which is~~ → use "of" directly

### 🎯 **Stress Patterns:**
- All CAPITALIZED words = stressed (louder/clearer)
- Numbers: Always stress FIRST part (SEVENTY-two, THREE hundred)
- Pauses: Use "|" marks as natural breath points (0.3-0.5 seconds)
- Link words: "At the TOP" flows as "AttheTOP" in natural speech

### 📊 **Common Number Pronunciations:**
- **SEVENTEEN hundred** = "SEV-un-TEEN HUN-dred" (NOT "one seven zero zero")
- **TWO thousand and SEVEN** = "TOO THOU-zund and SEV-un"
- **TWENTY-five to THIRTY-four** = "TWEN-tee FYVE to THUR-tee FOR"
- **SIXTEEN point TWO percent** = "SIK-STEEN POYNT TOO pur-SENT"

## 🎤 Practice Tips

1. **Natural Flow**: Don't pause after every word - let phrases flow together
2. **Stress for Emphasis**: Use ALL CAPS words for natural emphasis (not shouting)
3. **Link Words**: "At the TOP" sounds like one unit, not three separate words
4. **Variable Content**: Speak `[bracketed content]` clearly but naturally
5. **Practice Full Answers**: Don't practice individual words - practice complete sentences
