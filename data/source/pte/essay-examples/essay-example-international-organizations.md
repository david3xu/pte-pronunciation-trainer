# PTE Essay Example - International Organizations & Global Problems

## Topic
> Global problems are related to governments and international organizations. What are the problems and what is your solution?

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 291** | **Template: B**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of global problems and the role of governments and international organizations has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that addressing these global challenges requires both identifying key problems and implementing coordinated solutions, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling problem facing governments and international organizations is their potential to struggle with coordination and resource limitations. In particular, global issues such as climate change and poverty require collaborative efforts, yet different countries and organizations often face challenges in reaching consensus and allocating sufficient funding, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as delayed action and incomplete solutions. For instance, evidence from policy research reveals that approximately 80% of international initiatives experienced coordination difficulties as a direct result of conflicting national interests and limited resources.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that effective solutions may also give rise to positive changes. This is largely because strengthened cooperation between governments and clear frameworks established by international organizations are likely to generate unified strategies and shared responsibilities, which may subsequently cause global problems to be addressed more effectively. According to research by international relations studies, over 60% of successful global initiatives have been able to achieve significant progress through collaborative approaches stemming from regular communication and binding agreements among nations.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that while governments and international organizations face serious coordination challenges, implementing stronger cooperation mechanisms and clear action frameworks represents viable solutions for global problems, as the influence of both unified efforts and structured collaboration is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for world leaders and international bodies to dedicate adequate resources and political commitment to managing this issue effectively.
