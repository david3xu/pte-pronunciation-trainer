# PTE Essay Example - Smartphone Invention and Social Impact

## Topic
> In our technological world, the number of new inventions has been evolving on a daily basis. Please describe a new invention and explain the impact, either beneficial or detrimental, that you feel it has had on society.

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 272** | **Template: B** | **Method: Vocabulary File Only**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of modern inventions and their technological impact on society has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that smartphone technology represents one of the most important inventions of recent decades with both beneficial and detrimental effects, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of smartphone technology is its potential to facilitate improved communication systems and access to digital technology. In particular, smartphones enable instant connectivity through online platforms and information technology, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as enhanced productivity and greater access to innovative solutions for daily challenges. For instance, evidence from technology research reveals that approximately 80% of people rely on smartphones for essential communication systems as a direct result of technological advancement in mobile devices.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that smartphone technology may also give rise to certain challenges for society. This is largely because excessive smartphone usage and constant digital connectivity are likely to generate negative effects on mental health and face-to-face social interaction, which may subsequently cause people to experience reduced personal connections and increased dependence on digital technology. According to research by social studies, over 60% of smartphone users have been affected by diminished interpersonal communication stemming from over-reliance on online platforms rather than direct human interaction.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that smartphone technology represents a revolutionary innovation with substantial influence on modern society, as both the positive effects of enhanced communication and the negative effects of social disconnection are far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for individuals and communities to dedicate adequate attention and balanced usage to managing this technological development effectively.
