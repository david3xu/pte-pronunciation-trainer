# PTE Essay Example - Tourism in Less Developed Countries

## Topic
> For a less developed country, the disadvantages of tourism are as great as the advantages. Please discuss this statement, and give and explain your opinion.

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 271** | **Template: B**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of tourism in less developed countries has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that while tourism brings significant advantages to less developed countries, the disadvantages can be equally substantial, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of tourism is its potential to facilitate economic growth and job creation. In particular, tourism in less developed countries can foster increased income through visitor spending and employment opportunities in hospitality sectors, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as improved infrastructure and better living standards for local populations. For instance, evidence from development studies reveals that approximately 80% of less developed countries with tourism experienced economic benefits as a direct result of tourist revenue and related business activities.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that tourism may also give rise to significant disadvantages. This is largely because tourism development in less developed countries is likely to generate environmental damage and cultural disruption, which may subsequently cause local communities to face challenges such as resource depletion and loss of traditional lifestyles. According to research by sustainable development institutions, over 60% of tourism destinations in less developed countries have been affected by negative impacts stemming from overcrowding and exploitation of natural resources.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that the statement holds considerable validity, as both advantages and disadvantages of tourism in less developed countries are substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for governments and tourism planners to dedicate adequate resources and careful planning to managing this issue effectively.
