## Template 1 Phrases (In Order)

1. This pie chart | /ðɪs paɪ tʃɑːt/ — sounds like **this PIE chart**
2. This bar chart | /ðɪs bɑː tʃɑːt/ — sounds like **this BAR chart**
3. This line graph | /ðɪs laɪn ɡrɑːf/ — sounds like **this LINE graf**
4. shows information about | /ʃəʊzˌɪnfəˈmeɪʃən əˈbaʊt/ — sounds like **SHOWZ-in-fur-MAY-shun uh-BOUT** (linked: show**s͜i**nformation)
5. Looking at the data | /ˈlʊkɪŋ æt ðə ˈdeɪtə/ — sounds like **LOOK-ing at the DAY-tuh**
6. the highest number appears in | /ðə ˈhaɪɪst ˈnʌmbə əˈpɪəz ɪn/ — sounds like **the HY-ist NUM-bur uh-PEERZ in**
7. close to | /kləʊstuː/ — sounds like **KLOHSS-too** (linked: clos**e͜t**o)
8. Following that | /ˈfɒləʊɪŋ ðæt/ — sounds like **FOL-oh-wing THAT**
9. comes second | /kʌmzˈsekənd/ — sounds like **KUMZ-SEK-und** (linked: come**s͜s**econd)
10. with about | /wɪðəˈbaʊt/ — sounds like **with-uh-BOUT** (linked: wit**h͜a**bout)
11. The lowest can be found in | /ðə ˈləʊɪst kæn biː faʊnd ɪn/ — sounds like **the LOH-ist kan bee FOWND in**
12. at only | /ætˈəʊnli/ — sounds like **at-OHN-lee** (linked: a**t͜o**nly)
13. also feature in | /ˈɔːlsəʊˈfiːtʃərɪn/ — sounds like **AWL-soh-FEE-chur-in** (linked: featur**e͜i**n)
14. this chart | /ðɪs tʃɑːt/ — sounds like **this CHART**
15. Overall | /ˌəʊvərˈɔːl/ — sounds like **oh-vur-AWL**
16. the data tells us about | /ðə ˈdeɪtə telzʌs əˈbaʊt/ — sounds like **the DAY-tuh TELZ-us uh-BOUT** (linked: tell**s͜u**s)
