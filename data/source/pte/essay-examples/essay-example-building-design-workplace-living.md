# PTE Essay Example - Building Design Effects on Work and Living

## Topic
> How does the design of buildings affect, either positively or negatively, where people work and live?

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 268** | **Template: B** | **Method: Vocabulary File Only**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of building design effects on where people work and live has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that architectural features exert substantial influence on both living environment and working environment conditions, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of well-designed buildings is their potential to facilitate improved quality of life for people choosing where to work and live. In particular, natural light access, ventilation systems, and thoughtful spatial design can foster positive effects such as enhanced environmental quality and better physical health, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as increased productivity in working environments and greater satisfaction in residential areas. For instance, evidence from urban planning studies reveals that approximately 80% of people prefer well-designed buildings with adequate public spaces as a direct result of improved living environment conditions.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that poorly designed structures may also give rise to certain challenges affecting where people work and live. This is largely because inadequate spatial design and limited physical infrastructure are likely to generate negative effects on environmental quality, which may subsequently cause people to avoid certain residential areas or commercial districts. According to research by architectural studies, over 60% of workers report reduced productivity in working environments when building design effects restrict natural light access and proper ventilation systems.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that building design plays a vital role in determining where people choose to work and live, as the influence of architectural features and environmental quality is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for urban planners and developers to dedicate adequate resources and attention to creating well-designed buildings that support both working environments and residential areas effectively.
