## Example 6: Early Marriage

### **Question:**
> "It is argued that getting married before finishing school or getting a job is foolish. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

### **Question Type:** Agree/Disagree

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 1):**

**Main topic:** early marriage  
**Paraphrases:** marrying young, this decision, getting married early, early commitment

**Subjects:** young people, young couples

**Positive outcomes:** emotional stability and support, family responsibilities and money management, personal growth and stable relationships

**Negative outcomes:** financial difficulties and less freedom, reduced education and career opportunities, economic challenges and limited job growth

**Idiom:** "settle down" (for establishing family life)

**Stakeholders:** governments and communities

---

### **Complete Essay (233 words)**

The question of **whether marrying before completing education or employment is unwise** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **early marriage offers substantial benefits** for **young people** despite **certain limitations**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **early marriage** is its potential to bring about **emotional stability and support**. In particular, **marrying young** can facilitate **family responsibilities and money management skills**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **personal growth and stable relationships**. **Young couples who marry early** demonstrate **stronger emotional bonds and greater life satisfaction**. For instance, **partners who commit to marriage at a young age** report significantly **better emotional support and relationship stability** compared to those **who delay marriage significantly**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **getting married early** faces certain challenges in **modern society**. This is largely because **limited financial resources** create **financial difficulties and less freedom**, which then lead to **reduced education and career opportunities**. **Many young people** who **settle down before completing their education** experience pressure that creates **economic challenges and limited job growth**. As a result, many **young couples** have **difficulty achieving financial independence** that make it difficult to **maintain this early commitment successfully**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of **early marriage**, since its advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany it. Thus, it is essential for **governments and communities** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.

## Example 12: Building Design Impact

### **Question:**
> "How does the design of buildings affect, either positively or negatively, where people work and live?"

### **Question Type:** Advantages and Disadvantages (framed as "how does X affect")

### **Fill-in Terms (from B1 Example 11):**

**Main topic:** building design  
**Paraphrases:** architectural planning, building layout, structure design, space planning

**Subjects:** people, workers, residents

**Positive outcomes:** natural light and comfortable spaces, better productivity and improved mood, fresh air and open layouts

**Negative outcomes:** poor air flow and small spaces, health problems and lower motivation, stress and discomfort

**Idiom:** "cramped quarters" (for small, uncomfortable spaces)

**Stakeholders:** architects and developers

---

### **Complete Essay (236 words)**

The question of **how architectural planning influences work and living environments** has attracted considerable attention in modern society. I contend that **building design offers substantial benefits** for **people** despite **certain limitations**, and the following analysis outlines the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of **good building design** is its potential to bring about **natural light and comfortable spaces**. In particular, **architectural planning** can facilitate **better productivity and improved mood**, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as **fresh air circulation and open layouts**. **Workers in well-designed buildings** demonstrate **higher satisfaction and better performance**. For instance, **employees in buildings with natural lighting and open spaces** report significantly **improved well-being and focus** compared to those **in poorly designed facilities**.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that **building design** faces certain challenges when **planning is inadequate**. This is largely because **poor architectural choices** create **insufficient ventilation and limited space**, which then lead to **health problems and lower motivation**. **Many people** who **work in cramped quarters with poor air quality** experience pressure that creates **physical discomfort and reduced productivity**. As a result, many **residents and workers** have **difficulty maintaining health and efficiency** that make it difficult to **function effectively in poorly designed spaces**.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate the overall importance of **building design**, since its advantages remain significant despite the issues that accompany it. Thus, it is essential for **architects and developers** to provide sufficient support to address these challenges effectively.
