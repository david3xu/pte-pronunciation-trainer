## 🔥 MOST CRITICAL PRONUNCIATION RULES FOR #11-20

### Rule 1: Large Numbers with Decimal Points
- ✅ "TWO point FOUR billion" (stress both numbers)
- ❌ "two-point-four billion" (run together - wrong!)

### Rule 2: Price Expressions
- ✅ "ONE hundred and FORTY dollars" (stress all numbers)
- ❌ "one-hundred-forty dollars" (no pauses - wrong!)

### Rule 3: Ratio Expressions
- ✅ "TWENTY-THREE to TWENTY-FOUR" (four stresses)
- ❌ "twenty-three-to-twenty-four" (flat - wrong!)

### Rule 4: Geographic Names
- ✅ "bay-JING" (stress second syllable for Beijing)
- ❌ "BAY-jing" (stress first - wrong!)

### Rule 5: Time References
- ✅ "TWELVE noon" (two words, both stressed)
- ❌ "twelve-noon" (run together - wrong!)

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES IN IMAGES #11-20

### Mistake 1: Complex Numbers
- ❌ "one-thousand-five-hundred" (run together)
- ✅ "ONE thousand FIVE hundred" (clear pauses, multiple stresses)

### Mistake 2: Temperature
- ❌ "tem-per-CHURE" (wrong syllables)
- ✅ "TEM-pur-uh-chur" (4 syllables, stress FIRST)

### Mistake 3: Kilowatt/Megawatt
- ❌ "kilo-WATT hours" (wrong stress)

### Mistake 4: Time Periods (AM/PM)
