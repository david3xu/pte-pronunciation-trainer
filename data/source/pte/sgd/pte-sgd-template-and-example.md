# Summarize Group Discussion (SGD) Template & Example

## Strategy Guide: How to Pick Terms

To use this template effectively for any SGD task, follow these rules for selecting your **8 terms per speaker**.

### 1. Selection Criteria (The "Easy" Rule)
*   **Pick only what you hear clearly:** If you didn't catch it 100%, skip it.
*   **Strict Transcription:** Use the **exact words** from the transcript. Do not simplify or change them.
*   **Avoid proper nouns:** Names of people or specific places (like "Honey Cup") can be tricky. Use generic terms like "new place", "coffee shop", "student", "teacher" instead.
*   **Short or Natural Phrases:** Can be 2-5 words. Use the full natural phrase if it's easy to say (e.g., "give back to the community" is better than just "community").
*   **Easy to Pronounce:** Avoid complex or tongue-twisting words (e.g., choose "initial trust" over "skepticism" if possible, or pick a different phrase).
*   **Chronological Order:** Try to pick terms in the order they are spoken. This helps you keep track.

### 2. Filling the Template
The template requires **8 slots** for each speaker. Fill them in this specific order:

**Speaker 1:**
1.  **[Explanation 1]** (Start of speech)
2.  **[Explanation 2]**
3.  **[Mention 1]** (Middle of speech)
4.  **[Mention 2]**
5.  **[Highlight 1]** (Key point / opinion)
6.  **[Highlight 2]**
7.  **[Agreement/Last Point 1]** (End of speech / reaction)
8.  **[Agreement/Last Point 2]**

*Tip: If a speaker speaks multiple times (turns), continue adding terms from their later turns into the later slots (6, 7, 8).*

### 3. Handling "Agreement"
The last sentence for each speaker is: *"Then, he also **agree** with [Term 7] and [Term 8]."*
*   This does NOT strictly mean they "agreed" in the text.
*   It is just a template structure to fit the last two terms.
*   Just plug in the **last two terms** you heard from that speaker into these slots.

This discussion is between three speakers, they are talking about **[Main Topic]**.

According to the first speaker, he begin by explaining **[Point 1]** and **[Point 2]**. Following this, **[Point 3]** and **[Point 4]** were also mentioned. After that, the first speaker highlighted about **[Point 5]** and **[Point 6]**. Then, he also agree with **[Point 7]** and **[Point 8]**.
