# PTE Essay Example - Building Design Effects

## Topic
> Do you think the design of buildings affects, positively or negatively, where people live and work?

**Target Score: 25-26/26** | **Words: 274** | **Template: B**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of building design and its impact on living and working environments has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that the design of buildings significantly affects where people live and work in both positive and negative ways, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling positive effect of building design is its potential to facilitate comfort and productivity. In particular, well-designed buildings with natural light and good ventilation can foster improved wellbeing and enhanced work performance, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as better quality of life and increased job satisfaction. For instance, evidence from architectural research reveals that approximately 80% of people in well-designed workplaces experienced higher productivity levels as a direct result of thoughtful building design features.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that poor building design may also give rise to negative effects. This is largely because badly designed buildings with limited space and inadequate facilities are likely to generate stress and health problems, which may subsequently cause people to avoid living or working in such environments. According to research by urban planning studies, over 60% of individuals have been affected by discomfort in poorly designed buildings stemming from insufficient natural light and poor air quality.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that building design plays a vital role in determining where people choose to live and work, as the influence of both positive features and negative limitations is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for architects and urban planners to dedicate adequate attention and resources to managing this issue effectively.

## Special Note: "Positively or Negatively" Questions

**When a prompt asks about positive or negative effects:**
- Template B structure is PERFECT! ✅
- Paragraph 2 = Positive effects (advantages)
- Paragraph 3 = Negative effects (challenges)
- This naturally balances both sides
- Use "positive" / "positively" words in Para 2: well-designed, improved, enhanced, better
- Use "negative" / "negatively" words in Para 3: poor, badly, inadequate, insufficient

**Simple contrast words:**
- Good buildings vs Bad buildings
- Well-designed vs Poorly designed
- Positive effects vs Negative effects
- Comfort vs Discomfort
- Improved vs Inadequate
