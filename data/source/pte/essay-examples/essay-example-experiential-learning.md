# PTE Essay Example - Experiential Learning

## Topic
> Some people point that experiential learning (i.e. learning by doing it) can work well in formal education. However, others think a traditional form of teaching is the best. Do you think experiential learning can work well in high schools or colleges?

**Target Score: 26/26** | **Words: 271** | **Template: B**

---

## The Essay

In recent years, the topic of experiential learning in formal education has emerged as a prominent subject of public and academic discussion. I contend that experiential learning can work well in high schools and colleges when combined with traditional teaching methods, and the following analysis presents the primary arguments supporting this viewpoint.

To begin with, one compelling advantage of experiential learning is its potential to facilitate practical skills and deeper understanding. In particular, learning by doing can foster active engagement with real-world problems and hands-on experience, ultimately leading to broader outcomes such as improved critical thinking and better knowledge retention. For instance, evidence from educational research reveals that approximately 80% of students in experiential programs experienced enhanced learning outcomes as a direct result of practical application in classroom settings.

However, it is equally important to acknowledge that traditional teaching methods may also give rise to certain valid arguments. This is largely because structured lectures and textbook learning are likely to generate solid foundational knowledge through proven teaching approaches, which may subsequently cause educators to view traditional methods as more reliable than experimental approaches. According to research by educational institutions, over 60% of successful students have been able to achieve academic excellence through traditional teaching stemming from systematic instruction and clear learning frameworks.

Taken together, these considerations demonstrate that both experiential learning and traditional teaching have merit for high schools and colleges, as the influence of practical experience and solid foundations is substantial and far-reaching. Thus, it is imperative for educational institutions to dedicate appropriate resources and planning to managing this issue effectively.
